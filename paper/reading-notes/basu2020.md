## 核心命题（原文）

1. **输入结构 = 高阻 DDA 积分器直连传感器（SC 采样不在输入端）**（p.1–3）："we propose to implement the first integrator by a DDA based FD CT R–C integrator… input signal (Vip, Vin) from sensor is directly fed to non-inverting input pair of the DDA, while the R–C feedback-network (for integrator functionality) is connected at inverting input terminals… the input signal source remains isolated from the R–C network (in contrast to reported R–C integrator based CT ΔΣMs [6,19]) ensuring balanced high impedance for the FD input, which is an important requisite of IA."（p.3）——**CT 首级无采样开关 → 输入端无 SC 踢回；SC 采样只发生在次级（采首级积分器输出）**。这是「输入结构决定踢回来源」的正反面教材。
2. **踢回/振铃的最强段落：SC 开关电流 → bond-wire 寄生电感 → 参考电压振铃 → RC 阻尼网络（值靠仿真定）**（p.8）："Since the buffer loop-bandwidth is finite, its output impedance tends to increase at higher frequencies. Also, the generated voltages being used in SC circuitry, incur transient glitches and noise due to high frequency switching. To minimize such effects, large off-chip bypass capacitors (Cext) ~ 20 μF have been used… However, parasitic inductance of the package bond-wires (required for connecting the external capacitors) forms an under-damped parallel resonant circuit with on-chip capacitances [34]. Thus, whenever there is a current variation in the bond-wires due to switching in the SC circuits, the reference voltages tend to ring. To diminish such oscillations, parallel on-chip R–C damping networks as shown in Fig. 11(c) have been utilized [33]… The values of RD and CD have been suitably set using simulation for minimizing the settling time of ringing."（**SC 电路开关造成 bond-wire 电流突变 → 参考电压欠阻尼振铃 → 加片上并联 RC 阻尼网络；RD/CD 的取值"用仿真设定以最小化振铃建立时间"——正是 H5「外置 RC 网络的 R 值选择受回稳约束」的域内实例，且明确承认无解析取值、靠仿真**）
3. **比较器踢回与隔离（kickback 一词的直接出处）**（p.7）："The devices M1A & M1B also provide adequate isolation of the input terminals from kickback disturbances from regeneration nodes [31] at the beginning of reset and regeneration phases."（再生节点对输入端的踢回扰动，靠器件隔离）；"A pre-amplifier may be additionally used before the dynamic latch to improve the comparator resolution while reducing the offset and kick-back from the regenerative latch. But, even without it, the topology is capable of achieving the required resolution, hence saving power [32]."（预放大器是标准减踢回手段，本文为省电不用）
4. **建立约束的两处显式判据**（p.4, p.5）：
   - 运放带宽："The UGF of the opamp of CT first stage is required to be of the order of fs in order to avoid incomplete (yet linear) settling at the integrator output causing increased IBQN [6,19]."（UGF ~ fs，避免"不完全但线性"的建立抬升带内量化噪声）
   - 斩波扰动："chopping has been done at fch = fs. Further, chopping operation is performed at the middle of the sampling clock phases as illustrated in Fig. 6(d). This provides enough time for the DDA to settle from disturbances (at both the input and output ports) as caused by chopping, and thereby prevents sampling of chopper spikes by next integrator stage."（**把斩波沿放在采样相中点 = 给 DDA 留半个周期从扰动回稳**——「半周期内回稳」约束的时序化实现）
5. **CT 首级的固有抗混叠主张（P3/H3 域）**（p.4）："The low-pass nature of the CT first stage integrator enables inherent anti-aliasing property by attenuating out-of-band high frequency interferers before sampling occurs at the input of the DT second stage. In addition, as the signal is sampled at a frequency much higher than its bandwidth, so, the requirement of an explicit AAF preceding this system becomes redundant."（CT 积分器低通本性 = 固有 AAF；显式 AAF 冗余）另 p.2 CT ΔΣM 优点清单含 "better noise immunity due to inherent anti-alias filtering, absence of kT/C sampling noise"。
6. **输入对共模失配 ±10% 容忍（P1 域旁证，仿真非解析）**（p.5）："Another aspect is the probable input CM mismatch between the DDA input pairs gmi and gmf. However, it has been verified from simulation that there is no appreciable effect on the system performance for a mismatch of up to ±10% between the respective CM levels."（两输入对 CM 电平失配 ±10% 内性能无可觉影响——**仿真验证，无解析条件**）
7. **电荷注入的对策（延迟下降沿）**（p.6）："non-overlapped clock phases φs and φs along with corresponding delayed versions φsd and φsd have been used… Delaying of only the falling edges is sufficient for minimizing error due to signal dependent channel charge injection from the switches [6,7]."（只延迟下降沿即可压信号相关沟道电荷注入）

## 关键数值（原文）

- 整机（摘要 p.1；Table I p.9）：0.18 µm CMOS、1.8 V（可 1.2 V）；信号带 1 kHz；fs=1 MHz、OSR=500；仿真 SNR 80.1 dB（含噪声，13.0 ENOB）、无噪声 92.6 dB；实测 SNR 比仿真 "lower by about 9 dB"（≈71 dB，Table III：70.8/70.9 dB @ −3 dBFS）；功耗 98.4 µW @1.8 V、66.4 µW @1.2 V
- DR："The designed AFE can detect from a minimum input level of 0.001 mV to a maximum level of 300 mV, hence, yielding an overall DR of 109.54 dBFS with respect to full-scale amplitude of 300 mV."（p.10，仿真值，仪器限制未能实测）
- 首级积分器（p.6, §4.3）："a sampling frequency of 1 MHz has been chosen yielding an OSR of 500. Thus, from (2), RC = 2Ts = 2 µs for which R = 100 kΩ and C = 20 pF have been used."（RC 由 ωUGF,CT = (1/RC)·G = ωUGF,DT = G/Ts 匹配定出，式(2) p.3）；"For a second order ΔΣM, maximum OSR of about 256 is enough… a somewhat large fs has been deliberately chosen in order to reduce the RC value"（fs 大是为缩 RC 面积）
- 次级 SC 积分器（p.6）："capacitors of the order of 1 pF are adequate"（Ci/Cf=0.5 定增益）；CMFB 电容 "CS = 20 fF and CC = 100 fF"，"Having CS bigger (~5x) than CC results in quicker settling of the output CM after start-up… But, the settling time at each clock cycle will be more."（p.5）
- 时钟（p.8）："The nominal delay and non-overlapping times are 2.5 ns and 3 ns respectively, while the rise and fall times are around 40 ps."
- 参考电压链（p.8）：外部旁路 Cext ~ 20 µF；缓冲输出阻抗 "of the order of a few ohms"（运放+源跟随器闭环）；阻尼网络 RD/CD 数值未给（仿真设定）
- 噪声（Fig.7 p.6）：10 Hz 处 8.8 mV/√Hz（无斩波）→ 14.3 µV/√Hz（斩波）→ 8.5 µV/√Hz（斩波+差分 gm）——**不同 gm 技术把输入电阻噪声按 gmi/gmf 比例缩小**
- Monte Carlo（p.10）：100 样本失配仿真 "showing only minor variation in performance due to mismatches"
- PVT（Table II p.10）：SNR 79.4–83.5 dB、功耗 89.5–111.2 µA 跨角

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照

- **P1（失配消去条件，G_DC≡0）——相关性：低（一处旁证）**。p.5 的「DDA 两输入对 CM 失配 ±10% 内无影响」是**仿真验证的容忍度**，非「失配→共模增益」的解析条件；Monte Carlo 也只是性能波动统计。方向上与 P1 无冲突（它不关心无源差分网络）。killRisk：无。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）——相关性：无**。高阻输入 DDA 直接接传感器（p.3），无两线/四线引线结构讨论。killRisk：无。
- **P3（平坦度约束衰减上界）——相关性：低（固有 AAF 路线证人）**。p.4 "inherent anti-aliasing property… explicit AAF preceding this system becomes redundant" 与 p.2 CT ΔΣM 优点清单是「CT 首级免显式 AAF」主张的标准表述（H3 域）；**无衰减量数值、无平坦度概念**。killRisk：无（它支持「AAF 可以不是独立 RC 级」的立场，不触及 P3 的解析上界）。
- **方法学（D1–D10，H5 域）——相关性：高**：
  - **D9（kickback 回稳）**：p.8 的「SC 开关 → bond-wire 振铃 → RC 阻尼、RD/CD 仿真定值」是**外置/无源网络取值受回稳约束的域内直接证据**，且其「靠仿真最小化振铃建立时间」恰是 P1 论文门槛化判据（D9 解析化）可以超越的现状；
  - **D5（建立）**：两处判据——UGF~fs（p.4）与斩波沿置采样相中点留半周期回稳（p.5）——「半周期内回稳」约束的两种工程化形式；
  - **C9（运放峰值）**：bond-wire 电感 + 片上电容构成**欠阻尼并联谐振**（p.8）正是 C9「运放峰值/振铃」的封装级来源；其解法（RC 阻尼 + 大 Cext + 低阻缓冲）是完整对策链；
  - 比较器踢回（p.7）与 SC 输入踢回是两回事，引用时区分。
- killRisk 总判：**无**。
