## 核心命题（原文）

1. **主命题：DDA + 数字增益搜索，最大化增益同时保线性摆幅**（p.819）：
   > "Utilizing a differential difference amplifier (DDA) in combination with a digitally assisted gain selection, the gain is maximized during each readout while the amplified sensor signal is kept within the linear output range of the DDA. The readout consumes 290 µA with a measured resolution of 22 µV at maximum gain. For the presented sensors an in-plane shear stress or differences of normal stresses as low as 24 kPa can be resolved at a full scale signal of 100 MPa, while non-mechanical contributions are suppressed."（Abstract, p.819）
   中文：用"读出链自动门槛搜索"代替固定设计余量——与 D 系门槛建模同一设计哲学（按实测状态自适应设定工作点）。

2. **传感器是四管惠斯通电桥：共模类扰动（全桥同变）被结构性抑制**（p.819）：
   > "Both stress sensors are based on four MOSFETs with a length of 10 µm and a width of 5 µm connected in a Wheatstone bridge configuration with a common gate contact. The output signal is a differential voltage and the influence of physical effects affecting the parameters of all four FETs equally, such as the absolute temperature, is suppressed."（p.819）
   中文：电桥对称性=对"共模类"物理量的结构性抑制（这里是温度，不是电 CMRR）——差分测量主线的传感器端范例。

3. **高阻读出要求：不得从桥臂抽取电流（否则破坏线性）→ 需差分高阻输入级**（p.820）：
   > "To conserve the high linearity of the sensor elements, cross currents out of the sensor should be avoided. Hence, a high ohmic readout architecture is required. Additionally, a differential input stage is needed as the sensors generate differential signals due to the Wheatstone bridge architecture. A differential difference amplifier (DDA) is chosen as it offers better offset and linearity performance compared to conventional instrumentation amplifiers due to an improved matching potential of the input stage."（p.820）
   > "With this DDA architecture an open-loop DC gain of 90 dB and a GBW of 3 MHz was realized with a current consumption of less than 100 µA [1]."（p.820）
   中文："源阻抗高→读出必须不加载"约束与 P1 场景（高阻节点、AAF 不容许直加重载）同构；DDA 的双差分输入对（桥接正对、反馈接负对）是差分读出的另一种编排。

4. **干扰按"角序"分离：四向电流切换 + 数字傅里叶分量提取（0 阶=失调、1 阶=温度梯度、2 阶=应力）**（p.821）：
   > "As seen in (1) and (3)-(4), several offset contributions of the sensor are of different angular order than the signal due to mechanical stress. Therefore, it is possible to separate these signals using a discrete Fourier transformation of the signal V⊥,sum obtained from a measurement with fourfold switched current."（p.821）
   > "V⊥,σxy = (V⊥,1 − V⊥,2 + V⊥,3 − V⊥,4)/4　(5)"（p.821）
   > "Further contributions remaining after DCS are other signal components of 2nd angular order, i.e. geometrical offsets of the sensor and residual stresses. These offsets are device specific; they have to be measured and saved in advance within a calibration process."（p.821）
   中文：把不同物理成因的误差调制到正交"通道"再数字分离——与斩波/相关双采样同族，是 D 系门槛建模可借鉴的**误差域分离**技术。

5. **增益阶梯设计：T 网络电阻比闭式 + 二叉搜索树**（p.820）：
   > "The gain is digitally selectable in 16 steps from 1 to 271 by a resistive T-network [5]."（p.820）
   > "ACL = (R/r)² − 33(R/r) + 271　(2), with R/r ranging from 0 to 15."（p.820）
   > "If the amplified sensor signal is out of the DDAs linear range, the gain is decreased ('over-range'), otherwise it is increased ('in-range'). The borders of the range were set to 364 mV and 3.041 V according to simulation results."（p.820）

## 关键数值（原文）
- 灵敏度：NMOS σxy −990 µV/MPa；PMOS σxx 910 µV/MPa；非线性 <0.1% FS（NMOS ±30 MPa、PMOS 0–100 MPa）（p.820）。
- 传感器工作点：共模电压 726 mV（NMOS）/2.597 V（PMOS）；电流 209/47 µA；Vbias=3.3 V（p.820）。
- 读出：16 档增益 1–271（R/r 0–15）；ADC 10 bit SAR、3.22 mV 分辨率；范围界 364 mV/3.041 V；fchop=10 kHz；1/f 角 1 kHz（NMOS）/400 Hz（PMOS）（p.820–821）。
- 整机：290 µA @3.3 V（960 µW）；面积 0.294 mm²；数字噪声 1.9 LSB；分辨率 22 µV@增益 271 → **23.6 kPa（NMOS）/24.8 kPa（PMOS）**；0.35 µm CMOS XH035（X-FAB）；传感器尺寸 30×30 µm²（p.821–822）。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件）**：相关度 **无**。无源差分滤波器/消去条件完全缺席；差分只出现在"电桥+DDA 输入对"层面。killRisk：**无**。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）**：相关度 **无**。
- **P3（平坦度约束衰减上界）**：相关度 **无**。
- **方法学（D1–D10）**：相关度 **中（旁证）**。三点可借用：①"自适应门槛（增益搜索）代替固定余量"——与门槛化方法学同构；②"按角序/调制域分离误差成因再数字提取"——误差分域分离的模板；③"高阻源不得加载读出"的约束表述方式。应用背景（压阻桥差分读出、正畸智能托槽遥测供电）与 DE-DRMS 的双电极高阻测量同为"高阻差分源+低功耗读出"家族，可作引言域引文。
- **killRisk 总评：无**。
