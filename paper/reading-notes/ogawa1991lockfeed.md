## 核心命题（原文）
1. **原理：两个电容中存储的馈通电荷相消**（p.1，摘要）：> "Novel switched-capacitor circuits are presented which greatly suppress the clock-feedthrough effect. The principle is based on the cancellation of feedthrough charges stored in two capacitors. The circuit is also insensitive to parasitic capacitances and offset voltages of the opamps, and thus allows accurate analogue signal processing."（电荷域相消 + 对寄生电容与运放失调均不敏感——与 Martin 1982 的"正输入注入法"是同一问题域的另一种消去结构。）
2. **问题定位：开关电荷注入是 SC 电路头号误差源**（p.1）：> "Now that the parasitic-insensitive, gain- and offset-compensated stages have solved the problems associated with the CMOS opamp, the charge injection accompanying a switch is the most serious error source that degrades the signal-to-noise ratio in switched-capacitor circuits."（增益/失调补偿成熟之后，剩下的就是开关 kickback——为 P1 的"kickback 是 SC 输入的固有约束"提供文献背书。）
3. **对既有方法的划界：哑管只消交叠电容项、全差分要求平衡输出**（p.1）：> "This method is effective in cancelling the charge due to the overlap capacitances between the gate and source/drain, but not for the signal dependent charge stored in the channel of a main switch. The signal-independent cancellation can be realised by a fully differential configuration, but it requires additional components and opamps with balanced outputs."（通道电荷的信号相关分量是哑管法盲区；全差分是"信号无关消去"但代价是额外运放与平衡输出——P2 的差分/Kelvin 思路在此以"成本换共模抑制"的面目出现。）
4. **消去条件 = 元件匹配 + 时序条件**（p.1）：> "The feedthrough charges stored in C1 and C2 are opposite in polarity, but equal in magnitude if M1 and M3 consist of matched transistors and are turned off quickly."（M1/M3 匹配是元件条件，"turned off quickly"是时钟斜率条件——注意这不是纯元件条件，与 P1 的 G_DC=H_N−H_P≡0 纯传输函数条件有本质差别。）消去成立位置：> "the offset voltage due to clock feedthrough does not appear at the output because the feedthrough charges stored in C1 and C2 cancel each other. Neither parasitic capacitances nor the offset voltage of an opamp have an effect on the operation."（p.1）
5. **同一原理可复用到 S/H 与积分器**（p.1–2）：> "Fig. 2 shows the clock-feedthrough compensated sample/hold circuit. All the capacitors can assume any value providing that the holding capacitor C3 is much larger than C1 and C2."（p.1）；> "They are also applicable to filters because inverting and noninverting integrators can be formed by the adder/subtractor and the sample/hold circuit."（p.2）

## 关键数值（原文）
- **实验平台为分立器件**（p.1）：> "the noninverting amplifier shown in Fig. 1a was built using an LF356 opamp and MC14016 analogue switches. Two 30 pF capacitors were used for C1 and C2."（LF356 + CD4000 系 MC14016 模拟开关，C1=C2=30 pF；不是 MOS IC 单片实现。）
- **无补偿输出失调 ≈200 mV，补偿后降到"可忽略"**（p.2）：> "The offset voltage about 200 mV can be seen generated by the clock-feedthrough charge. ... It can be seen that the offset voltage is reduced to a negligibly small level, independent of the signal amplitude."（输入为峰值 100 mV 正弦；补偿后残余**未给数值**，只有波形照片 Fig. 3；垂直刻度 200 mV/div。）
- 时序方案：φ2、φ3 是 φ1 的延迟副本，φ0 为 φ3 的非交叠补（p.1，"easily generated by simple logic gates"）；C3 用于缓解压摆率要求（> "capacitor C3 is incorporated to alleviate the slew rate requirement"，p.1）。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件 = 元件条件）**：同为"消去条件"家族，但 Ogawa 的条件是**电荷域匹配 + 关断速度条件**（依赖时钟沿斜率），并非纯元件传输函数恒等式；且消去发生在电路内部两电容之间，与 P1 的"外置无源差分网络使 G_DC=H_N−H_P≡0"不同层。提供的是谱系支撑，非竞争。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）**：文中明确全差分是信号无关消去的正路但代价高（> "requires additional components and opamps with balanced outputs"，p.1），可作 P2 差分结构成本论证的旁证；无 Kelvin/地板量化内容。
- **P3（衰减上界）**：无衰减上界概念，无关。
- **增量四（门槛化）**：无建立/回稳门槛；仅有 C3"缓解压摆率要求"的定性设计考虑。
- **killRisk：无威胁**。理由：①片内/模块级 SC 电路补偿技术，不涉及外置 RC 网络与 ΣΔ 输入驱动；②其消去依赖主动时序改造（改开关顺序），P1 是无源网络传输函数条件；③残余指标未定量化，不构成对 P2/P3 数值主张的覆盖或反驳。
