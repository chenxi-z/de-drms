## 核心命题（原文）

1. **【答②·方法总纲】** 本书限直流、0.01% 级："the methods discussed are those regularly used at this Bureau when an accuracy of 0.01 percent or better is required.... Those presented are comparatively simple, being based largely on substitution procedures, yet they are capable of yielding results of high accuracy."（Preface, p.ii）
2. **【答②·电位计法（比率测压法）】** "if a suitable standard resistor is available, it is unnecessary to calculate the current through the unknown resistor. If the same current flows through the known and the unknown resistor, the ratio of the potential differences across the two is the same as the ratio of the resistances. Hence [eq (7)] X = S·Vx/Vs"（p.12，公式 OCR 糊、按正文语义排版）。优势与代价同页明说："It has an advantage over the ordinary Wheatstone bridge in that the resistance in terms of which the unknown is measured may be that of an actual standard resistor instead of one of the coils of the bridge."（p.12）；"The potentiometer method, however, is more difficult to use as the currents through the potentiometer and in the measuring circuit must both be kept constant, whereas the balance of a Wheatstone bridge is independent of the current flowing through it."（p.12）。"A type of measurement for which the potentiometer method is especially well suited is for the measurement of four-terminal resistors, which are parts of complicated networks"（p.12）。
3. **【答②·比率/替代法】** "In nearly all measurements where the highest possible accuracy is desired, a substitution method is used. That is, the change required to restore balance after replacing a standard with an unknown is measured."（p.20）；放大机制："If the unknown and standard differ by 0.1 percent, the difference need be determined to only 1 percent to give the unknown to 10 parts in a million in terms of the standard."（p.14）；"It is evident that the calibration of the measuring bridge is of least importance when the standard and unknown resistances are nearest equal."（p.14–15）。
4. **【答②·匹配条件理论——有，且是全文最硬的一段】Kelvin 双电桥：辅助比对准主比，链路电阻项恒等消去**：eq (11)（p.17，公式由页面图像复原）**X = S·A/B + [b(A/B − a/b)/(a+b+L)]·L**；正文紧接："This equation would be exactly the same as that for a simple Wheatstone bridge if the last term on the right side were zero or negligibly small as compared with the term S A/B. That last term can be made zero by making a/b = A/B, irrespective of the value of the link resistance, L. However, the smaller the value of L, the less important is any lack of equality between the ratios A/B and a/b."（p.17）——**"匹配后误差项与寄生参数取值无关"** 的消去条件（a/b=A/B ⇒ 第二项恒为零，不论 L 多大），与 P1 的主张结构同型；同页还给了平衡判据 "This bridge is balanced if there is no change in the deflection of the galvanometer when the current circuit is opened or closed."（p.17）。简单惠斯通平衡式印刷本体：eq (8) X/S = A/B，eq (9) X = S·A/B（p.13，图像复原）。
5. **【答②/④·引线匹配条件】** "In work of the highest accuracy, account must be taken of the resistance of the leads... A better arrangement is to make the lead resistances adjustable and select their ratio in such a way as to balance out their effects."（p.18）；公式条件 eq (12)–(14)：(A+La)/(B+Lb)=a/b 除 "La and Lb are respectively negligible as compared with A and B, or under the condition that the ratio of the leads is the same as the ratio of A to B. That is (A+La)/(B+Lb) = A/B if La/Lb = A/B"，且 "If the lead resistances are small as compared with A and B, the second equation need be only approximately satisfied."（p.18）；辅助臂同样处理："Their effect may be made negligible by making their ratio the same as the ratio a/b."（p.18）；五步平衡终态 eq (16)–(18)："X/S = A/B = a/b = La/Lb"，"X = SA/B = Sa/b"（p.19）。
6. **【答④·另一个匹配不变性句】** "If we could select leads such that the ratios x/y and x'/y' were the same as X/Y, the ratio of the two arms would be independent of the actual values of the lead resistances. That is, the balance would be the same as if the resistances of the leads were negligibly small."（p.23）。
7. **【答②·交换对称消差（与匹配互补的第二条路线）】** Kohlrausch 重叠分流法："Under either condition of adjustment, S and X are equal... The balance is exact even if the two circuits of the galvanometer are not exactly alike electrically or magnetically."（p.13）；Mueller 桥引线互换："Instead of adjusting Lx and L4 to exact equality, their connections are interchanged and a second bridge balance is obtained, the average of the two balances being that which would be obtained with Lx and L4 equal."（p.15）；双替代法："the resistors are interchanged, and a new balance is obtained. The percentage difference between the two resistors is half the difference between the two readings of the ratio set."（p.21）；四端比较的三读数加权平均（数值例：5497、5613、5835 → (5,613+5,497)/2+10/11(5,835−5,613) = 5,757，p.24）。
8. **【答②·稳定性与温度处理】** 材料："precision resistors of good quality usually can be obtained with temperature coefficients in that interval of not more than 10 ppm/°C."（p.3）；"Temperature coefficients of resistance change little with time, and if changes do occur in their values there is very probably an accompanying large change in resistance."（p.3）；老化："the coils are usually baked at this temperature [150 °C] for 48 hours... In the two or three months immediately following their baking, resistance coils will ordinarily increase in resistance by an amount that is usually of the order of 0.01 percent."（p.6）；湿度机理（Dike）："the effect of changes in humidity is to change the tension in the silk with which the wire is customarily insulated. This change in tension changes the pressure transmitted to the wire by the insulation and hence changes the resistance."（p.7）；测量期温度纪律："In comparing resistances by the substitution method, the two balances of the ratio set should be made quickly so that temperature changes will not cause variations in the ratio set between readings. It is best to have all coils of the ratio set made from the same lot of resistance material. If this is done and manganin coils are used, a change in temperature of 1 deg C should not change the ratio more than 2 or 3 parts in a million."（p.21）。
9. 温度工程手段（p.8–9、15）：NBS 型油浸密封（Rosa）、双壁干空气密封（"Probably the most stable resistors that have been made are a group of annealed 1-ohm manganin resistors mounted in double-walled air-filled containers"，p.7）、Mueller 桥恒温仓 35 °C（p.15）、恒温箱高于室温除湿（p.9）。
10. **【答②·电桥校准】** eq (21) 带修正项计算 X；"The factor (1+a−b) is an approximation for (1+a)/(1+b) and is accurate if a and b are small as compared with unity. If neither a nor b exceeds 0.001, the error from neglecting the second-order terms does not exceed two parts in a million."（p.29）；温度计桥等臂互换免校准："With such interchangeable ratio arms, actual calibrations of them are unnecessary. The calibration of a thermometer bridge requires only a calibration of the rheostat arm."（p.29）。

## 关键数值（原文）

- 锰铜：84 Cu / 12 Mn / 4 Ni，ρ = 45–50 µΩ·cm，对铜热电势 2–3 µV/°C（p.2）；R-T 抛物线 eq (4) R_t = R_25[1+α(t−25)+β(t−25)²]，α ≤ 10×10⁻⁶，β = −3…−6×10⁻⁷，峰两侧 10 °C 内比峰值低 30–60 ppm（p.3）；峰在 20–50 °C，max–min 差约 1¼%（p.3）
- 金铬合金（Au+2% Cr）：20–30 °C 内总变化 "did not exceed a few parts in ten million"，热电势 7–8 µV/°C（p.4）
- 时间稳定性：基准组相互比较年变化 ≤1–2 ppm（p.10）；1941 年 NBS 对近 600 只送检标准统计：不计符号年均变化 **8 ppm**，近 90% 年变化 ≤10 ppm，仅 2% > 60 ppm/年；计符号则密封 ≈ **−0.3 ppm/年**、非密封 ≈ **+5 ppm/年**（p.10）
- 负载系数瞬态：1 W → 线圈 30 s 内高出线轴约 1 °C，随后 10–15 °C/h 爬升，约 2 h 后高出室温 10–20 °C，永久变化仅几 ppm（p.9）
- 比率组（DRRS）：固定臂 100 Ω + 99.445 Ω + 三盘 0.1/0.01/0.001 Ω，比率步进 0.1/0.01/0.001%，插值 0.0001%，读数以 ppm 标注（p.19–20）；并联分流盘使开关接触影响按电流比**平方**衰减（100:1 → 0.01 Ω 接触变化仅 1 µΩ，p.16；另一例 36:1，p.20）
- 替代比较能力：10 Ω–1 kΩ 二端标准比对到 **1–2 ppm**；<10 Ω 汞杯接触电阻致困，故改四端（p.21）
- Mueller 桥：可调臂步进 0.0001 Ω，比率臂 A=B=1000 Ω，开关汞齐化仅数 µΩ 接触不确定度（p.15–16）
- 惠斯通桥：比率臂取 10–100 Ω 折衷，避免 <10 Ω 或 >1 kΩ、可调臂读数 >1 kΩ，最佳量程 10–10,000 Ω；0.01% 级需加修正 + 温度维持几度内 + 频繁校准（冬夏比率臂与可调臂各变 0.01% 以上，p.14）
- 1:4 比率用 5 只近等电阻轮换取平均："they must not differ by more than 0.1 percent if the average of the five ratios is to be correct to one part in a million"（p.25）；引线电阻须成 1:4（p.25）；n 只并联/串联比恰为 1:n²（p.25–26）
- 通用比率组（URS）：总阻约 2,111 Ω 恒定，电位点分辨 0.01 Ω（p.26）
- 退火铜电阻率国际值 1.7241 µΩ·cm @20 °C（p.32）

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照

- **P1（失配消去条件）**：本书 #4–#6 就是**前电子时代的"匹配条件理论"完整版**：a/b=A/B ⇒ 链路电阻 L 无论多大都被消去（p.17）；La/Lb=A/B ⇒ 引线影响消失（p.18）；x/y=x'/y'=X/Y ⇒ 平衡与引线实际阻值无关（p.23）。P1 论文应把这三条列为最近先例并点明 delta：**全部是直流电阻比值匹配，无相位、无 RC、无共模概念**（全文 grep "mismatch / common-mode / ladder" 零命中）；P1 的对象是差分 RC 网络失配致 CM→DM 转换（频率相关、双端口），Thomas 的对象是桥臂数值比（频率无关、单频零位）。另注意 #7 的交换取均路线：经典解法优先"换位消差"而非"配元件"，这是 P1 novelty 论证的一部分。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）**：四端定义的规范表述在本书："the resistance is equal to the ratio of the potential difference between the potential terminals P, P to the current flowing in and out the current terminals C, C."（p.5；p.13 同句复述）；"Methods of measurement are used such that any effect from the lead resistances is avoided or reduced to a negligible amount. For such a purpose the potentiometer method is suitable as no appreciable current is drawn through the potential leads"（p.13）。四端/二端的选择准则（接触电阻 ≈0.0001 Ω：>1 Ω 可忽略、0.01 Ω 时 1% 误差，p.13）是 P2 结构类论证的第一手材料。
- **P3（AAF 衰减上界）**：无。全书直流为主，仅 p.32 末提测液体电导须用交流桥且 "This requires a balance of reactance as well as of resistance when the cell is measured in an alternating current Wheatstone bridge."。
