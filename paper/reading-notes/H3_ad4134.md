## 核心命题（原文）

1. **输入是纯阻、无采样保持（① "易驱动"原文）**："Due to the CTSD architecture, the AD4134 has a pure resistive input, with a simplified input structure diagram, as shown in Figure 65. The ADC supports only fully differential input signals. The input impedance has a differential resistance value of 6.25 kΩ. Internally, both AINx+ and AINx− are biased to VREF/2 through the internal resistor network."（p.37）
   "CTSD architecture allows the AD4134 to have a constant resistive input characteristic. This behavior simplifies the front-end circuit design, allowing lower bandwidth, and low power high performance precision amplifiers to directly drive the ADC."（p.30）
   "Other than being easy to drive, the other major advantage of the CTSD architecture is its inherent antialiasing property."（p.31）
   "Because of the easy to drive nature of the AD4134, the op amps do not need to have a high bandwidth and a strong output drive to overcome kickbacks from traditional ADCs."（p.62）
2. **开关电容扰动机理（④ 手册版）**："The first is the sudden loading of the input node by the sampling capacitor, for which the magnitude of the disturbance is proportional to the input differential voltage/differential time. The second impact is from the charges stored in the parasitic capacitance of the switches being pushed out to the input node when the switch is closed, a phenomenon known as charge injection or charge kickback. In either case, the sudden change of current flow at the input of the ADC reacts with the finite impedance of the driving circuit to create a disturbance in the form of voltage variation."（p.30）
3. **内在抗混叠的机理 + 边界**："Without the sample-and-hold circuit, the sampling of the analog signal takes place inside the CTSD modulator at the quantizer, after the integrator. This sampling scheme allows the device to take advantage of the low-pass response of the integrator and intrinsically reject signals around the sampling frequency of the modulator. This property provides an inherent aliasing rejection of up to 102.5 dB for the AD4134."（p.31）
4. **调制器时钟固定**："One unique property of the CTSD modulator architecture is having a fixed time constant. As a result, the AD4134 device operates at a fixed modulator clock frequency."（p.39）

## 关键数值（原文）

- **输入参数（Table 1，p.6）**："Input Current 317 µA/V"（typ）；"Input Current Drift 8.3 nA/V/°C"（typ）；"Differential Input Resistance 6.25 kΩ"（typ）；"Input Common-Mode Voltage Range (VCM)"= VREF/2 至 AVDD5/2（p.37："from VREF/2 to AVDD5/2. - **AAREJ（②）**：定义（Terminology，p.28）："Alias rejection is defined as the ratio of the power in the ADC output at frequency, fIN, to the power of a −6 dBFS input signal at frequency, MCLK ± fIN. Alias rejection = 10 log(PfIN/PMCLK ± fIN)"；

The other antialiasing mode, AA2, improves the rejection to 102.5 dB with the cost of a higher offset drift of 1.03 µV/°C, additional power consumption of 3 mW per channel, and higher noise level with dynamic range reduction."（Table 35：DR 107.4→105.9 dB、SNR 106.6→105.4 dB、Offset Drift 0.5→1.03 µV/°C、126→129 mW/ch）

## ① 专项：模拟输入引脚外部 RC / EMI 滤波推荐（P1 "CTSD 门后残余网络"证据）

**负结果（全文检索证实）：AD4134 数据手册没有、也没有推荐任何模拟输入引脚外部 RC/EMI 滤波器。** 证据链：

- AINx± 引脚描述只有一句（p.17，四通道句式相同、仅通道号不同）："Positive Analog Input to ADC Channel 0."，无任何外挂元件要求；
- 输入结构图 Fig.65（p.37）只有片内 RIN/CIN（"In Figure 65, CIN means input capacitance and RIN means input resistance."），**注意 CIN 只画了符号，全文未给数值**；
- Front-End Design Examples 的四条要求（p.62）逐条是："1. Provide adequate input impedance to match the source. 2. Provide reasonably low output impedance to drive the 6 kΩ differential input resistance of the ADC. 3. Convert the input signal to a balanced, fully differential signal with fixed common-mode voltage of 2 V to 2.5 V. 4. Provide the necessary gain or attenuation to match the maximum source signal amplitude to the full-scale input range of the ADC."——**无 RC 滤波项**；Quick Start Table 20 "Requirements to Operate the AD4134"（p.45）的 Input Stage 也只列放大器（AD8421, ADA4075-2, ADA4945-1, LTC6363, LTC6373），无 RC；
- **手册里唯一一句 "RC circuit is required at the ADC input" 是在讲离散时间 ADC 的对比电路**（Fig.63）："An additional RC circuit is required at the ADC input to ensure stability of the driver and to help further suppress the kickback."（p.32）——它属于 "Example Analog Front-End Circuit Design of the Discrete Time-Based ADC"（Fig.63 图题），**不是给 AD4134 的推荐**；AD4134 自己的 Fig.64 注解反而是 "the instrumentation amplifier can directly drive the resistive inputs of the AD4134, and the bandwidth of the amplifier adds to the antialias rejection, making the signal chain an alias free signal chain."（p.32）；
- 全书 "EMI" 仅两处，均与模拟输入无关：电源 "LT8606 or LT8607 provide a low EMI, small size solution"（p.59）；时钟布线 "without the need of routing high-speed clock lines that may add to EMI issues"（p.61）。

**"门后残余网络"的实际形态（换芯片后残余问题）**：CTSD 门后只剩①片内 6.25 kΩ 分压偏置网络（p.37）；②用户自己引入的串联电阻与源阻抗——即 tutorial p.22 的 Rs 分压增益误差（见 `H3_adi_ctsd_tutorial.md`），手册 p.62 同义句为 "Provide reasonably low output impedance to drive the 6 kΩ differential input resistance of the ADC"。残余网络**不再承担抗混叠职责**（"The AD4134 is fully protected from the out of band frequency tones"，p.31），也**不承担 kickback 抑制职责**（④：纯阻输入无 kickback），因此厂商连 EMI 小 RC 都不给——这正是 P1 里"CTSD 门后滤波网络可删到什么程度"的最强权威边界证据。

## ⑤ 专项：无源差分网络失配 → 共模消去条件（P1 反例搜索）

**负结果：全文没有**（检索 mismatch/matched/CMRR/common-mode 全命中已核）。最接近的三处：

1. FDA 内部电阻网络匹配 → CMRR（p.63）："Devices such as the LTC6363-0.5, LTC6363-1, and LTC6363-2 with a highly matched integrated resistor network offer unmatched CMRR at 94 dB minimum."＋"This circuit can also provide gain or attenuation of the signal and is responsible for rejecting the input common mode."（p.63）——**只给器件级 CMRR 数值，不给失配→CMRR 退化公式**；
2. ADC 自身差分口 CMRR（Table 1，p.8）："Common-Mode Rejection Ratio (CMRR) 100 mV p-p on VCM with no decoupling capacitor / DC 78.4 dB / AC Up to 10 kHz 74.5 dB"；
3. 高阻源 + 受控共模时"不需要"共模消去（p.62）："An example of a high impedance source includes a Wheatstone bridge type of configuration… The input common mode is well controlled, needing no common-mode rejection, and a dual op amp configuration works properly."

即：与 Whitlock 的平衡桥谱系相反，AD4134 语境里共模处理被**外包给前端放大器**（in-amp/FDA），无源网络失配→共模转差模的条件在本文档不存在；这符合 P1 预期（该条件需回 Whitlock/差分 AAF 文献找）。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照

- **P1（"CTSD 门后残余网络" / 失配消去条件）**：本手册是"残余网络最小化"的实证上限——输入脚外**零**推荐 RC（见上专项），残余物仅片内 6.25 kΩ 分压与用户源阻抗。它同时把 P1 的失配对象**转移**了：换 CTSD 芯片后，误差源从前端 RC 失配/相位失配（tutorial p.19 的 ±0.15°@1% RC）退化为"Rs 与 RIN 的分压比 + 温度系数差"（tutorial p.22），后者是可校正的增益项而非结构性消去问题。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）**：p.62 要求 2 "drive the 6 kΩ differential input resistance" 与 P2 直接对话——源阻抗与 6.25 kΩ 的分压构成 P2 式分辨率地板；但因为 RIN 是**已知固定值**（6.25 kΩ typ，手册未给容差，见"没读到的"），地板可预算、可校正，这点比两线采样有利。
- **P3（AAF 衰减上界）**：AAREJ 的测试窗口（MCLK ± 160 kHz，−6 dBFS 单音）就是"内在抗混叠保证区"的实证边界；窗口外靠数字滤波（stop band 110 dB @ 0.499×ODR，p.7）+ 调制器低通。P3 若讨论"AAF 衰减上界"，本文档提供的是**分段保证**（片内模拟 ~85/102.5 dB @ fs 附近 → 数字 110 dB），而非单级上界。
- **引文价值**：p.30 的双机理 kickback 段 + p.37 纯阻输入段是"开关电容 vs CTSD"对比的标准引文；p.8/p.28/p.56 三处 AAREJ（条件/定义/两档）构成②的完整证据；p.6/11/56/87 的相位匹配四件套（规格/测试注脚/延迟微调/寄存器）覆盖③。

## 没读到的

- **MCLK 本身的 rms 抖动规格**：手册只给晶振 ±100 ppm（p.8）、外部时钟 47.9–48.1 MHz（p.8）、ODR 脚 ≤100 ns p-p（p.41）和 Fig.22 曲线（无轴值可引）；"10 ps rms"量级只见于 tutorial p.31（"the order of requirements would be like ±100 ppm accuracy in frequency and rms jitter of 10 ps"），**数据手册无对应表格项**。
- **输入电容 CIN 数值**：Fig.65（p.37）画了符号但全文无值；"Input Capacitance 10 pF"（p.8）属于 CLKIN/ODR/DCLK 数字脚，勿误引到 AIN。
- **RIN 6.25 kΩ 的容差/温漂**：只给 typ，无 min/max、无漂移系数（Input Current Drift 8.3 nA/V/°C 可反推但手册未明写）。
- **内部一致性存疑（引用时注意）**：VCM 脚 50 Ω 串联电阻的容性负载门槛，引脚表写 "larger than 0.2 nF"（p.17），正文写 "larger than 0.1 µF"（p.37）——同一要求两个数值，需以 ADI 勘误/实测为准。
