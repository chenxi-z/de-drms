## 核心命题（原文）
1. **历史正典：变压器比率臂电桥是数字时代之前阻抗比对的唯一手段——P2 点名的"结构性比率"产业对应物**（p.3）：
   > "Before the introduction of digital technologies, this operation could be performed only by means of transformer-ratio bridges, where an impedance ratio is compared with a voltage or current ratio generated by a transformer [8, 9]."
   （阻抗比对 = 阻抗比 vs 变压器生成的电压/电流比——比率由结构产生而非元件值。）
2. **数字电桥仍是"比率由电压比给出"，平衡零示条件下残差被剥离**（p.3–4）：
   > "In these fully-digital impedance bridges, an impedance ratio is determined by a voltage ratio generated by either an electronic polyphase synthesizer or two Josephson arbitrary waveform synthesizers (JAWS)."
   完整模型（Eq.1，p.3）含检零残差项；完美平衡时（p.4）：
   > "and the impedance ratio deduced from (1) is simply given by Z2/Z1 = U2/U1."（Eq.2）
   （平衡条件 UD1=UD2=0 时比率退化为纯电压比——检零+残差项线性化是这类电桥的方法学核心，与 P1 的 G_DC=0 零示条件同构。）
3. **4TP 定义 + 三重连接是阻抗"被定义"的前提**（p.3）：
   > "In the following, all relevant impedances are defined as four-terminal-pair (4TP) impedances [30], and the impedance of the ACQHR standard is defined by the well-known triple-connection scheme [31]."
   （4TP 定义引 Cutkosky 1964 [30]——正是 H7 轴已读的 Cutkosky 谱系。）
4. **电流均衡器与 Kelvin 平衡：结构上强制低电位端等电位**（p.3）：
   > "Two active equalizers are added in different network meshes of the impedance bridge to ensure current equalization"
   > "Next, the Kelvin balance will be carried out by adjusting amplitude and phase of the source SK to minimize the difference between the detector readings at LP1 and LP2."
   4TP 平衡判据（p.7）：
   > "The bridge is balanced and the impedances properly defined as 4TP impedances when VLP1 = 0, VLP1 − VLP2 = 0 and VDHP1 = VDHP2 = 0."
5. **正反换接取几何平均抑制系统误差**（p.4）：
   > "To compensate systematic errors in the bridge setup (e.g., connecting cables, imperfect grounding), the ratios were measured in forward (F) and reverse (R) configurations." … "The final ratio is given by the geometric mean of the forward and reverse measurements"
   JAWS 阵列电感误差同样被几何平均压制（p.4）：
   > "Overney et al [20] have shown that the error is strongly suppressed in the geometric mean of a forward and reverse ratio."
   漂移抑制（p.7）：
   > "In a series of repeated measurements, the balances are performed in the sequence FRRFFR. . . to partially cancel any first-order drift [39, 40] of the generated voltages due to temperature changes."
   （**换接对称化是对"结构对称性残差"的序列统计处理**——不对称本身就承认存在，见第 6 条。）
6. **残余不对称误差的诚实枚举**（p.7）：
   > "In practice however, when exchanging the standards, asymmetries in the two configurations can lead to different voltages at the high potential ports (e.g. because the current equalizers are not perfect or because of temperature changes between the forward and reverse configurations), thus causing a measurement error."
   Kelvin 网络误差项（p.4）：
   > "• Kelvin network (ΔK). The error of the Kelvin network affects the output values UD1 and UD2 . The error is determined when deliberately an improper current from the Kelvin network is injected to the bridge."
   （不确定度模型 Eq.5（p.4）：W + ΔW = (Uratio + ΔUJAWS + ΔUcable) × [1 + (M + ΔM) + (K + ΔK)]——ΔM=电桥偏差、ΔK=Kelvin 网络、ΔUcable=电缆，全是**结构残差项的显式记账**。）
7. **三角闭合自检：理想应为零，偏差 = 未计入系统效应**（p.8）：
   > "the triangle in figure 5 can be closed and the result should be zero in an ideal case. Any deviation from zero is a sign of unaccounted systematic effects."
   > "For the triangle measurements, the deviation measured with both impedance bridges is below four parts in 108 and for the INRIM–POLITO's bridge this can be assumed as zero within uncertainties. For the PTB's bridge, a deviation of two parts in 108 remains even when subtracting the uncertainties. This suggests unaccounted effects either in the ACQHR or in the bridge setup, especially for what concerns the cable correction and needs further investigation."

## 关键数值（原文）
- 不确定度（Abstract，p.2）："The uncertainties for the calibration of 10 nF capacitance standards at 1233 Hz are within 1 × 10−8 for the PTB's bridge and around 1 × 10−7 for the INRIM–POLITO's bridge. The comparison mutually validates the two bridges within the combined uncertainty."
- 电桥分辨力（p.4）："the input noise of the pre-amplifier (0.6 nV Hz−1 [32])"；"For a C : C measurement at 1233 Hz, this yields a Type A uncertainty of 3 nV V−1"（Allan 方差、典型测量时间 2 min）。
- JAWS 电压误差（p.4）：1233 Hz 串扰误差 "less than 1 nV V−1"；阵列电感 "(15.5 to 18.5) nH"、"linearly increasing with current and frequency"；kHz 段合计不确定度 "within the limit of 5 nV V−1"。
- 电缆修正（p.6，Eq.7）：ΔW_cable/W ≈ −½(Z_HP2·Y_HP2 + Z_LC2·Y_LC2 − Z_HP1·Y_HP1 − Z_LC1·Y_LC1 + …)（HP/LC 端电缆串联阻抗×并联导纳的半和修正；QHR 三重连接电缆参数对普通阻抗为零）。
- 频率/幅度（p.3）：Josephson 电桥同种阻抗比 53 Hz–50 kHz、rms ≤100 mV；下限 53 Hz 为避开 50 Hz 电源谐波。电子电桥（p.5）："The bridge operating frequency is from 20 Hz to 20 kHz"。
- 石墨烯 QHR（p.8）：p = 5.5 × 10^10 cm−2，μ = 5900 cm² V−1 s−1；工作点 B = 5 T、4.2 K、≈38 μA；ρxx = (−4 ± 24) μΩ；器件 400 μm × 约 1600 μm、6 触点、75 Ω 同轴引线。
- 实物标准漂移（p.8）：两 10 nF 标准漂移 < −2 nF F−1/日；12.9 kΩ 标准约 −2.26 nΩ Ω−1/日；频率依赖 0.145 μΩ Ω−1 kHz−1；恒温 30 °C、稳定性 mK 级。
- 结论定位（p.10）："The Josephson bridge has indeed an uncertainty which is an order of magnitude less than that of the electronic bridge"；电子电桥 "with an uncertainty of about one part in 107 (k = 1), suitable for primary impedance metrology"。
- 1:1 比约束（p.7）："This constraint can indeed be fulfilled only when |W| ≈ 1."

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件）**：相关度**低**。全文无 SC ADC、无抗混叠滤波器、无 RC 失配议题；频率为 kHz 段交流阻抗计量。方法学同构点仅一处：零示平衡（UD=0）下比率 = 纯电压比，残差以 ΔM/ΔK/ΔUcable 显式线性化入模型——即"理想条件 ≡ 零示条件，工程上以残差项记账"。可作 P1 写作时的旁证性类比，非证据。
- **P2（Kelvin 结构地板）**：相关度**高**。这正是 P2 所指产业级结构性比率计量的现役代表：4TP 定义（Cutkosky 谱系）、Kelvin 平衡网络（LP1−LP2=0 判据）、有源电流均衡器、同轴干扰免预结构（引 Awan/Kibble/Schurr [8]）。其量级定位清晰：结构化比率计量可达 1×10⁻⁸（≈−160 dB），远超 P2 的 ≈56 dB 地板——地板是"无主动平衡/无换接对称化的纯无源 Kelvin 结构"的下界，而本文展示加上检零反馈+正反换接后结构残差可再压四个数量级。P2 引用时须区分"结构定义正确性（4TP/Kelvin）"与"残余不对称的抑制手段（均衡器/几何平均/三角闭合）"两层。
- **P3（衰减可达上界）**：相关度**无**。
- **方法学**：三角闭合自检（三项比率乘积应为 1）与 FRRFFR 漂移抑制序列是门槛化证据设计的可移植范式。
- **killRisk 判断：无威胁**。领域（kHz 阻抗计量、量子标准）与 P1（SC ΣΔ 输入网络）零重叠；对 P2 是正向证据而非竞争性主张。
