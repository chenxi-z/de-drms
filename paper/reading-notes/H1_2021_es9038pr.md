## 出处

ESS Technology, Inc. ES9038PRO datasheet, revision 3.7, February 8, 2021。59 页。"SABRE® PRO Flagship 32-bit HypersStream® II 8-channel audio DAC"，64-eTQFP。面向 "Audiophile and Studio equipment applications"（p.1）。

## 核心命题（原文）

1. **性能口径（首页与 §ANALOG PERFORMANCE）**：
   - p.1："The ES9038PRO 32-Bit Audio DAC delivers an unprecedented **DNR of up to 140dB in mono mode and THD+N of –122dB**, the industry's highest performance level…"；Table（p.1）："DNR (dB) 140 (mono) / 132 (8-Ch)；THD+N (dB) −122"。
   - **THD+N 的测量口径原文（p.49）**："Test Conditions… 2. SNR / DNR: **A-weighted over 20Hz-20kHz in averaging mode** 3. **THD+N: un-weighted over 20Hz-20kHz bandwidth**"；"**THD+N (differential current mode) 0dBFS −122 dB**"；DNR：mono differential current mode −60dBFS **140 dB-A**；stereo 137；8-Ch 132 dB-A。
   - **模式对 THD 的影响**（p.1 FEATURES/BENEFITS）："Mono, stereo, or 8-channel output with either **current-mode or voltage-mode operation (current-mode gives lower THD)**"。

2. **片内 THD 补偿（本簇唯一的片内补偿件，§THD Compensation, p.11）**——原文：
   > "THD Compensation can be used to **minimize distortion from external PCB components and layout through the generation of inverse second and third harmonic components matching the target system distortion profile**."
   > "THD compensation can be enabled via **thd_enb in Register 13**… The coefficient for manipulating second harmonic distortion is stored in **Register 28-29: THD Compensation C2**. The coefficient for manipulating third harmonic distortion is stored in **Register 30-31: THD Compensation C3**."
   > "**All channels use the same compensation coefficients.**"
   寄存器定义（p.31）："A **16-bit signed coefficient** for correcting for the second harmonic"（thd_comp_c2）/ "…the third harmonic distortion."（thd_comp_c3）；Register 13 [6] thd_enb："Selects whether to disable the THD compensation logic. THD compensation is enabled by default and can be configured to correct for second and third harmonic distortion."；"1'b0: **enable THD compensation (default)**；1'b1: disable"（Register 13 定义页 p.27）。
   首页受益栏（p.1）："THD compensation — **Minimizes distortion from external PCB components and layout**"。
   **注意口径**：该补偿修的是"**外部 PCB 元件与布局**"引入的失真（即外围电路不对称/非线性的后果），系数需按"target system distortion profile"人工调；它**不是**对差分臂失配的自动校正，手册也没有说失配会被它吸收。

3. **输出/应用电路（§APPLICATION DIAGRAMS, p.45；Fig.1 Paralleled DAC Output, p.12）**——"镜像配方"证据：
   - p.45 图题原文："ES9038PRO 8-Channel Output, Current-Mode Operation / **SABRE DAC in 8-Channel differential in current-mode (DNR: 132dB, THD: −122dB)**"。目视核对（110 dpi 渲染）：DAC1/DAC1b 差分对各经 **U1/U3 I/V 级**（R1/R4 为两臂反馈电阻）→ R2/R5 → **U2 差分转单端**（R3/R6），输出 DAC1；两处 **VCM** 偏置点。**拓扑左右/上下镜像（R1↔R4、R2↔R5、R3↔R6 成对），但全图只有位号 R1–R6、无任何元件值、无电容**——ESS 给的是拓扑镜像，不给元件值配方。
   - p.12 并联输出原文："The ES9038PRO has a **low output impedance (typ. 202Ω)** and paralleling DAC outputs will further reduce the output impedance. Paralleling 4 channels as shown in Figure 1 will result in a **50Ω** output impedance. Paralleling 8 channels will result in **25Ω**. **Many op amps used for the I/V stage (U1 and U3) to drive such a low impedance will have difficulty maintaining low THD and linearity.** For the ES9038PRO, it is recommended to parallel DAC outputs after the output of U2 and use a set of op amps (U1, U2, U3) for all 8 channels."
   - 电源与 THD（p.11）："To minimize THD+N, **AVCC_L and AVCC_R must be powered by low-noise +3.3V supplies**… separate +3.3V supplies are highly recommended. The ES9311Q dual ultra-low noise regulator is designed to power AVCC_L and AVCC_R and minimize THD+N and crosstalk on all SABRE PRO DACs."

4. **增益校准 ADC（p.10）**——与"失配"最近的一段，但方向是**片间**而非臂间：
   > "DAC gain calibration enables uniform output level across multiple chips by **compensating for chip-to-chip gain variations**… The DAC full-scale gain-calibration system works by comparing an internal resistor to an external precision resistor of known value… **As all the DAC channels are on the same monolithic chip, the channel-to-channel gain variation is very small and does not need to be trimmed.**"
   ——即 ESS 认为片内通道间一致性质疑已被单片集成天然解决，只需片间校准；**未讨论外置差分网络失配**。

## 关键数值（原文）

- ANALOG OUTPUT（p.49）："Output impedance (RDAC) **202 Ω ±14%**"；"Voltage mode output range (VOPP) 0.924 × AVCC Vp-p"；"Voltage mode output offset (VOCM) Bipolar zero out **AVCC / 2**"；"Current mode output range 1000 × VOPP / RDAC mAp-p"。
- 电源（p.46 RECOMMENDED OPERATING CONDITIONS）：VDD_L/R = +1.2V ±5%（128 mA nom）；AVCC_L/R = +3.3V ±5%（90 mA nom）；"if… 8 outputs of the DAC are paralleled… and MCLK is greater than 80MHz, the VCC_L, VCC_R, and DVDD power supply will need to be increased from 1.2V to 1.3V"（Note 2；p.12 同文重述）。
- 复位（p.48）："the reset input should be pulled low for a minimum of 1ms after all external power supplies… are stabilized."
- 未用数字输入（p.5）："All unused digital inputs should be connected to ground directly, or via a pull-down resistor of **4.7kΩ to 47kΩ**"。
- IIR/PCM 滤波器特性表（p.49–50）：略（7 种预设 FIR + IIR 带宽档，与本簇问题无关）。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照

- **P1（失配消去条件）**：ESS 是本簇"镜像配方"的**输出侧原始件**，且比 Cirrus/AKM 更彻底——**连元件值都不给**（p.45/p.12 图仅 R1–R6 位号）。全文 grep：**mismatch / common-mode / CMRR / tracking 零次出现**（"common" 仅出现在 commonsense 之外的无）。失配议题最接近的是 p.10 片间增益校准（"chip-to-chip gain variations"）与 p.11 THD 补偿（修"external PCB components and layout"失真），**两者都默认对称性问题可由片内一致性 + 事后补偿处理，没有任何失配→共模转化的定量分析**。P1 反例搜索：**未找到反例，预期得到验证**。p.11 "All channels use the same compensation coefficients" 反而暗示补偿系数是**全局单值**、不区分臂——这与"臂失配需要逐对修"的机理相悖，可作讨论素材。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）**：无直接内容；p.12 的 202 Ω RDAC 输出阻抗与"低阻驱动难保 THD"一段是外围电路负载效应影响失真的旁证。
- **P3（AAF 衰减上界）**：无涉及（DAC 侧应用，滤波器章节只给数字滤波器响应）。
- **引文价值**：p.11 THD 补偿两句原文是"片内 THD 补偿"口径的标准引文（注意它修的是外部 PCB 失真、2/3 次谐波、系数人工调、全通道共用）；p.49 测试条件三行是"DTHD+N 测量口径"的标准引文（un-weighted 20 Hz–20 kHz、0 dBFS、differential current mode）；p.45 图可作"输出侧镜像拓扑不给值"的对照图。

## 没读到的

- 失配→共模转化定量分析：**没有**（mismatch/CMRR/共模字样全无）。
- S/(N+D) 随偏置变化：ESS 无此口径（DAC 无输入偏置问题；该口径属 AKM AK5578，见对应笔记）。
- p.45/p.12 应用图的元件值、I/V 级与差分转单端的设计方程、THD 补偿系数的标定流程（"matching the target system distortion profile" 一句之外无任何标定方法）。
- THD vs 频率/电平的逐点曲线（只有 −122 dB 单点规格）。
