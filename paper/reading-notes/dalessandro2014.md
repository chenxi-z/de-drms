## 出处
- A. D'Alessandro, F. Ubertini, A. L. Materazzi, S. Laflamme, M. Porfiri, "Electromechanical modelling of a new class of nanocomposite cement-based sensors for structural health monitoring," *Structural Health Monitoring*, 2014. DOI: 10.1177/1475921714560071. Univ. of Perugia / Iowa State / NYU。
- PDF 11 页 = 印刷页 1–11（Sage 排版，文字层完整；公式有排版粘连，仅作局部辨读用，不引用粘连段）。
- 定位：**H6 应用域（CNT-水泥基自传感，SHM）**——材料+集总电路建模文。测量链全部商品化 NI 硬件、两线制恒压直流测量；对 P1 有一处重要旁证（极化致直流电流衰减 + 接触电阻与体内电阻同量级）。

## 核心命题（原文）
1. **总命题：MWCNT 掺杂水泥基传感器（CNTCS）可用 R‖C+接触电阻的线性集总电路建模，应变调制体内电阻（p.1, Abstract）**：
   > "Here, we investigate the effectiveness of a linear lumped-circuit electromechanical model, in which dynamic sensing is associated with a strain-dependent modulation of the internal resistance. … Experimental results indicate that the lumped-circuit model is capable of accurately predicting the step response to a voltage input and its steady-state response to a harmonic uniaxial deformation."
2. **★直流极化漂移：恒压下电流随时间衰减（被测件不是纯电阻）（p.2）**：
   > "From an electrical perspective, cement-based materials primarily behave as dielectrics, whereby an imposed electric field induces the orientation of dipoles in the material so as to create an induced electric field opposite to the applied one. The resulting polarization is responsible for the decrease of the electric current over time under the application of a constant potential difference."
   （直流激励下电流以 τ≈4–6 s 衰减——**应用域里"直流下电阻读数会漂"的一手证据**，与 P1 讨论直流通道信息纯净度的语境直接相关。）
3. **★电阻模型三成分：体内电阻 + 电极电阻 + 接触电阻（p.2）**：
   > "In these models, the resistance of cement paste sensors is associated with the intrinsic resistance of the material, the resistance of the electrodes and the contact resistance between electrodes and cement paste. The capacitance is instead related to electric polarization."
   （接触电阻 Rc 是一等模型参数、且实测 ≈14.3 kΩ 与体内电阻同量级（Table 2, p.8）——**两线制下串联寄生不可忽略的应用域实证**；本文靠模型辨识而非 Kelvin 四线把它分离。）
4. **★测量链：恒压源 + 数字万用表测电流，两线制、同轴电缆防 EMI（p.5）**：
   > "the samples are connected to a power source providing a stabilized potential difference across their electrodes, V, and to a digital multimeter measuring the time trace of the current passing through the specimen … In order to minimize electromagnetic interference, coaxial cables are used to connect the power source, the sensor and the multimeter."
   （激励与测量在同一对电极上——两线/双端结构；EMI 对策止步于"用同轴线"，无共模/失配分析。）
5. **★测量硬件全部商品化（NI PXI）（p.6）**：
   > "National Instruments NI PXIe-1073 chassis is used to acquire data. A source measure unit, model NI PXI-4130, generates the stabilized potential difference V that is applied to the electrodes of the specimen through coaxial cables. The electrical current through the sample is measured using a high-speed digital multimeter, model NI PXI-4071."
   另（p.7）：> "The applied axial deformation is measured through two off-the-shelf resistive strain gauges attached on opposite lateral faces of the specimen, whose outputs are recorded via a data acquisition bridge input module, model NI PXIe-4330."
   （SMU + 高速 DMM + 桥式采集模块——**H6"测量环节全部商品化"的典型样本**，无自研前端、无前端误差预算。）
6. **四电极嵌入但两两选用（非 Kelvin 四线）（p.3, p.7）**：
   > "In each specimen, four electrodes are placed symmetrically along the central axis. Specifically, the two inner electrodes are placed at a distance of 2 cm, the external electrodes have a relative distance of 4 cm and the distance between an internal and an external electrode is 1 cm"（p.3）；
   > "all the measurements are performed with a common electrode, so that the following distances are investigated: d0 = 1, 3 and 4 cm."（p.7）
   （四根电极是为改变"活形体积"做参数化实验，每次测量仍是同一对电极上加压+测流——**不是外对通流、内对取压的四线 Kelvin 接法**。）
7. **超谐波现象：线性时变电路产生 n·v 谐波（p.7）**：
   > "equation (8) describes a harmonic distortion with respect to the monochromatic input, thus suggesting that the response of the sensor is characterized by superharmonic components generated by the sinusoidal axial strain. This phenomenon is not related to nonlinearities, but only to the time-varying nature of the governing linear differential equation."
   （建模→预测超谐波→FFT 实测验证的闭环，是"先预测可证伪特征再实测"的良好方法学示范。）

## 关键数值（原文）
- 激励与采样（p.7）：恒压 **V = 1.5 V** 直流；采样 1 kHz；阶跃试验每配置 1 h，谐波试验 600 s；16 组试验。
- Table 2（p.8，电极距 1/3/4 cm）：**Rc = 14.29 / 14.34 / 14.32 kΩ**（跨距不变→判接触电阻）；**R0 = 18.55 / 32.47 / 45.09 kΩ**；**C0 = 515 / 510 / 567 µF**；**τ = 4.16 / 5.07 / 6.16 s**。
- Table 3（p.9，应变计系数 λ）：**210.3–484.8**（随电极距与应力幅 0.15–0.30 MPa 变化；1 cm 处 λ 随应力幅明显增长 353.9→484.8）。
- 材料与试件（p.2, p.3, Table 1）：MWCNT 掺量 **2 wt%**（Arkema Graphistrength C100，>90% 碳，外径 10–15 nm）；水灰比 0.43；试件 50×40×50 mm³；不锈钢网电极插入约 75% 厚度。
- 力学（p.8）：纳米增强水泥切线杨氏模量 **≈20 GPa**（5 Hz 动载下）。
- **无任何 CMRR / 失配 / 噪声 / 前端精度指标数值。**

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件，G_DC≡0 元件条件）**：**中**。不触电路消去结构本身，但给两条应用域旁证：①被测 CNT-水泥复合材料直流下有极化电流衰减（p.2 + Table 2 τ≈4–6 s），"纯电阻被测件"假设在直流短时窗内不成立；②两线制下接触电阻 Rc(≈14.3 kΩ) 与体内电阻 R0(18.5–45.1 kΩ) 同量级且被当作一等参数辨识（p.2, p.8）——串联寄生在此域不可忽略，反衬消去/分离结构的必要性。
- **P2（Kelvin 结构地板，≈56dB）**：**低**。有四电极几何（p.3）但用法是两两选对的两端测量，非 Kelvin 四线；对结构地板/共模抑制零讨论。可作 H6 证据：**应用域通行做法是"商品化 SMU+DMM 两线测 + 模型辨识扣接触电阻"，而非四线硬件分离**。
- **P3（衰减可达上界）**：**无**。无滤波器/衰减内容。
- **方法学（门槛化）**：**低-中**。模型参数化（改变电极距）+ 参数一致性检验（Rc 恒定性）+ 可证伪预测（超谐波）+ FFT 多窗平均——"先模型后判据"姿态可取，但无门槛化判据。
- **killRisk 判断：无**。纯应用域建模文，与"对称性破坏/失配→共模差模转换"无接触；其价值是 C 级现状证据（测量全商品化 + 两线 + 直流极化漂移实据）。
