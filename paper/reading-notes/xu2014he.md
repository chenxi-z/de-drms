## 核心命题（原文）
1. **平坦度与衰减的"1 LSB"预算准则（配方线的理由核心）**（pp.170–171, §2）：
   > "In the pass band $0 < f < f_{h}$, the filter should have the flat amplitude-frequency characteristic; the fluctuation to the input signal should not exceed 1LSB of AD converter. In the stop band $f > f_{s} - f_{h}$, it requires that the filter has a sufficient attenuation, that is, the amplitude attenuation of signal should reach 1LSB of AD converter."
   （带内波动 ≤1 LSB、带外衰减到 1 LSB——把平坦度与衰减都锚定到 ADC 量化台阶，这是对 P3/D4 类门槛的"应用文版"表述；注意其对象是**单端逐通道**，不含差分两路匹配。）
2. **衰减预算公式：所需阻带衰减 = 6m − 20lg(Vs/Vn)**（p.172, §2 式(4)）：
   > "$-20\lg K_{n} = 6m - 20\lg\frac{V_{s}}{V_{n}}$ (4)"
   （m = ADC 位数，Vs = 传感器满量程、Vn = 最大噪声幅值；推导链：Vs·Kn = q 且 E=2^m·q。即衰减需求随位数升、随噪信比升而降——**配方线的定量理由，单通道口径**。）
3. **配方本体：模拟 + 数字分工，过采样放宽模拟过渡带 → 阶数从 12 降到 6**（p.171, §2 + p.173, §3）：
   > "In the new method, in order to lower the design requirements of analog filter, on the condition that Nyquist theorem, through appropriately increasing the sampling frequency and broading the transition zone, the order of filter which can achieve the equal stop band attenuation will reduce and the stop band ripple will decrease"（p.170–171）
   > "If setting that the stop band frequency of anti-aliasing filter is 430Hz, that means more narrow transition zone, then the order of filter will attain 12. So that the new method widens the transition zone, reduces the order, and makes the analog filter easy to implement."（p.173）
   > "In the transition band $f_{h} < f < f_{s} - f_{h}$, it does not require the filter has certain gradient."（p.171）
4. **模拟 AAF 不可省的边界论据（数字滤波器无法善后 fs−fh～fs 段）**（p.171, §2）：
   > "analog filter is essential. If the analog filter is not used, the high frequency signal in $f_{s} > f > f_{s} - f_{h}$ will alias to the frequency band $0 < f < f_{h}$ through digital sampling which can't be filtered effectively only by digital filter."
5. **具体配方：6 阶椭圆 = 三个二阶 Sallen-Key 级联（I 型×2 + II 型×1），RC 由 Filter Wiz Pro 生成**（p.173, §3）：
   > "Comprehensive consideration of the above mentioned, the elliptic filter is best choice for designing the anti-aliasing filter, which has favorable amplitude-frequency characteristic and phase-frequency characteristic in pass band from the point of approximation."
   > "The 6 order elliptic filter is designed and the parameters of RC are computed by the aided design software Filter Wiz Pro, which is implemented by three cascaded active filters... The circuit (a) and (b) are second order low pass filters with the structure of Salen-Key I. The circuit (c) is second order low pass filter with the structure of Salen-Key II."
   （注意：'椭圆滤波器通带有良好幅频**与相频**特性'一说存疑——椭圆逼近通带等纹波、相位非线性，此断言与其后 Fig. 6 '相位近似线性' 的自洽性**待核**；RC 具体取值文中未印出（只在图 4 电路图里，txt 无图）。）
6. **应用参数实例（表面测量链）**（p.173, §3）：
   > "If the pass band frequency is 400Hz, then the stop band frequency is 1300Hz... on the condition that a 12 bits AD converter is used, the maximum range of sensor is 10µm, and the resolution is 0.0025µm, setting the maximum amplitude of noise is 40 times the resolution, through the formula (4), the obtained minimum stop band attenuation should be -30dB."
   （采样率 1700 Hz（触针 0.85 mm/s、采样间隔 0.5 µm）；fs−fh = 1700−400 = 1300 Hz。**复算待核**：按式(4)，6m=72、20lg(Vs/Vn)=20lg(10/0.1)=40，得 32 dB，原文写 −30 dB（差 2 dB，疑取整口径）。）
7. **数字滤波收尾参数**（p.175, §4）：
   > "we design a FIR digital filter by FDA tool of Matlab to attenuate noise further. The pass band frequency of the digital filter is 400Hz, the stop band frequency is 430Hz and the minimum stop band attenuation is -30dB."

## 关键数值（原文）
- 信号界定：表面测量中 >400 Hz 的分量均属电器假信号，须滤除（p.172, §3 首段："the signals above 400Hz are false signals caused by electrical components"）。
- 采样链：截止波长 λc=0.8 mm、采样间隔 0.5 µm（每 λc 1600 点、7λc 共 11200 点）、触针速度 0.85 mm/s → fs=1700 Hz；传感器量程 10 µm、垂直分辨率 0.0025 µm；12 bit ADC（p.173）。
- 模拟 AAF：通带 400 Hz、阻带 1300 Hz、最小阻带衰减 −30 dB → 6 阶椭圆（3×Sallen-Key 二阶节）；若阻带压到 430 Hz 则需 12 阶（p.173）。
- 数字 FIR：通带 400 Hz、阻带 430 Hz、−30 dB、严格线性相位（p.175）。
- 预算公式：−20lg Kn = 6m − 20lg(Vs/Vn)（p.172 式(4)）。
- 全文无 R/C 元件值文本、无容差规格、无实测滤波器频响曲线数据（图 5/6 为软件绘制特性，图 8 为滤波前后轮廓对比）。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（G_DC=H_N−H_P≡0 消去条件）**：相关度 **无（空白佐证）**。单端单通道链路，无两路结构、无失配概念；其"1 LSB/−30 dB"预算全是标量单路口径——**配方线文献把 AAF 当单口器件处理**的又一实例，P1 的差分匹配消去条件在此类文献中无对应物。
- **P2（Kelvin 结构地板）**：相关度 **无**。
- **P3（平坦度约束下衰减可达上界）**：相关度 **中高**。本文是配方线中少见的**给了平坦度—衰减联合预算**的：带内波动 ≤1 LSB（平坦度门槛）+ 阻带衰减 = 6m − 20lg(Vs/Vn)（衰减门槛），并给出"放宽过渡带换阶数"的权衡实例（12 阶→6 阶）。可作 P3 的**应用文先例**引用；但注意其"上界"口径是需求侧预算（由 ADC 位数与噪信比决定），不是 P3 所说的"给定平坦度约束下滤波器可达衰减的物理上界"——两者方向不同（需求预算 vs 能力上界），引用时须区分。
- **方法学（D1–D4/D8）**：1 LSB@12 bit ≈ −72 dB 满量程口径，与 D1（≥80 dB 差模）不同源；−30 dB 阻带需求 << D1/D3，场景宽松得多；无共模内容（D8 不适用）。
- **killRisk**：**无**。不冲击 P1/P2/P3；P3 方向可引为"预算式配方"的正面先例（同时指出其无失配维度的局限）。
