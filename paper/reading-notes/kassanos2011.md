## 出处
- Kassanos P., Triantis I. F., Demosthenous A., University College London；2011 IEEE SENSORS Proceedings, pp. 327–330，DOI 10.1109/icsens.2011.6127367。
- PDF 共 4 物理页，页码 = 物理页。
- 内容：阻抗谱前端架构比较（SD/SS）+ 提出幅值/相位直接测量法（整流+低通取幅值，双比较器+XOR 取相位），分立件原理样机验证。

## 核心命题（原文）
1. **SD（同步检测）的误差机制：双通道失配 + 正交信号生成不准**（p.1 Abstract）：
   > "In contrast to synchronous detection (SD), which provides the real and imaginary components, the magnitude and phase of the impedance are calculated. Non-accurate generation of the required in-phase and quadrature signaals required in SD and mismatches between the channels generatee large errors. The proposed method does not require such signaals." [OCR损：字母重复为 PDF 文本层伪影]
   正文展开（p.2 §II）：
   > "Errors in the accuracy of this technique arise from mismatches between the two channels and most importantly in the generation of the 0o and 90o degree signals required for the demodulation."
2. **VCCS 的频变相移问题与解法位置**（p.2）：
   > "When a current is required to be applied, this is generated by a voltage controlled current source (VCCS), i.e. an operational transcon ductance amplifier (OTA) [10], which will introduce a phase difference between its input and output, at high frequencies. Hence, in order to reduce this, the demodulation signals should be generated following the OTA." [OCR损]
   （激励源自身相移污染参考通道——与本项目恒流源+比率测量的"激励链相位一致性"议题同源。）
3. **SS（同步采样）的精度绑定时序**（p.2 §III）：
   > "By sampling the measured potential difference precisely at the instance at which the injected signal reaches its peak value and crosses zero the real and imaginary parts, respectively, of the impedance can be obtained. The accuracy of this depends on the accuracy of the timing at which the samples are obtained."
4. **幅值/相位法的独立性主张（本文核心）**（p.3）：
   > "The calculation of the magnitude of the impedance is independent from the source signal, while the calculation of the phase difference is not. Thus, this technique does not rely that heavily on the synchronization between signals and should therefore produce more accurate results."
   （幅值测量与源信号解耦；相位仍依赖源——**解耦是部分的**，这一自认是诚实的方法学边界陈述。）
5. **相位测量的极简实现**（p.3）：
   > "As in phase-locked loops (PLL's) the phase can be very easily and accurately measured using simply a single XOR gate [14] with its inputs being two square waves, one synchronous to the injected signal and one synchronous to the measured signal. The gate output can then be low-pass filtered to obtain a dc output proportional to the duration of the time difference between the two pulses."
6. **SD 的不可替代优势（多频场景无需带通选频）**（p.3）：
   > "Nevertheless, it has to be highlighted that SD has one major advantage in contrast to all other methods. Since it is effectively a demodulation method it does not require bandpass filters for frequency selection in a multi-frequency scenario."
7. **所有方法的共同前提：输出为直流 → 电路须无失调**（p.3）：
   > "Finally, what should be pointed out is that in all methodologies, the outputs are dc signals and therefore the signal processing circuits used need to be offset-free. This was considered in [2] using the chopping technique in order to eliminate the offsets of the instrumentation amplifier used. Furthermore, as in chopping, the use of the four switches for the multiplication requires a fully differential design."
   SS 的消失调法（p.3）：
   > "In SS by taking four samples, two in each half cycle and by calculating the average of each pair, dc offsets can be eliminated [12]."
8. **单片集成的匹配优势论证**（p.1）：
   > "Integration of the required electronics on a single chip is desirable, as it would further enhance the advantages mentioned above and it would further increase the accuracy of the measurement as parasitic capacitances (i.e. from cabling) would be reduced. Additional advantages include better matching between identical building blocks and the integration of both the electronics and the electrodes on a single chip."
9. **对既有幅值/相位方案（AD8302 路线）的简化判据**（p.2 §IV）：
   > "Such an implementation, based on the AD8302 by Analog Devices was proposed in [13]. Division (subtraction in the logarithmic domain) of the potential across the load under test and that across a sense resistor (for calculating the current) was performed to obtain the magnitude. However, if a constant amplitude current source is used (e.g. a Howland circuit), then the current amplitude is known and its calculation and division of that with the measured potential (in the log-domain or not), is thus not necessary, as it is just a gain factor."

## 关键数值（原文）
- 验证结果（p.4 §VI）：
  > "In this example the load consisted of a 983.95Ω resistor in series with a 322.85nF capacitor with an impedance of 986Ω at the applied frequency of 500Hz. The multiplier output is amplified by a gain of 1.64. For the applied 3.2Vpk-pk input signal, the current was equal to 1mA. With real and imaginary parts of the impedance being approximately equal, the phase is about 45o (Fig. 6) and its magnitude is equal to 1,393Ω (Fig. 5)." [OCR损]
  > "The RC values were also measured using an impedance analyzer (Wayne Kerr 6500B). The normalized error between the impedance measured with the impedance analyzer and with this preliminary set-up was less than 0.2%."
  （数值自洽性核验：X_C = 1/(2π·500 Hz·322.85 nF) ≈ 986 Ω，|Z| = √(983.95² + 986²) ≈ 1393 Ω ✓——相位 ≈45° 与"实部虚部近似相等"一致 ✓。）
- 分立实现（p.3 §V）：仪表放大器 AD8250、乘法器 AD835（ADI）、双比较器 LM319（NSC）、两输入 XOR SN74AHCT86N（TI）；两只无源 RC 低通（"R=51Ω, C=470µF"）。
- 应用电流范围（p.1）："Applied currents may be in the range of several μA up to a few mA (~5mA rms in multi-frequency operation)."
- 生物传感安全幅值（p.1）："It has been reported that applied signals should not exceed 5mV in amplitude so that the functionality of the sensitive immobilized layers is not reduced [5]."
- 传感电极形态（p.1–2）："The sensors commonly used are two (the most common architecture being interdigital electrodes [8]) or three electrode systems [5]. The use of four electrodes has also been proposed [9]." [OCR损]

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件）**：相关度**低-中**。本文的"失配"对象是解调双通道与正交参考信号，非输入抗混叠网络；但其误差叙事与 P1 同构——SD 的精度被"两条支路的相等性"（通道匹配 + 0°/90° 参考生成精度）限定，而所提方法把幅值测量改为与源解耦的直接量（幅值独立于源信号），相位仍绑定源链。这为 P1 提供一个可引用的架构级旁证：**凡精度依赖两路信号相等性的方案，其误差即两路失配的读数；把比较改为对单一物理量的直接测量可消除该依赖**（但相位通道仍是依赖项——作者自己承认）。无 SC ΣΔ、无 RC 滤波器内容，killRisk 见下。
- **P2（Kelvin 结构地板）**：相关度**低**。仅提四电极方案引文 [9]，无 Kelvin/4TP 误差机制或地板量化。
- **P3（衰减可达上界）**：相关度**无**。
- **方法学**：单点验证（500 Hz、|Z|≈1393 Ω、误差 <0.2% vs 台式阻抗分析仪）作为 proof of concept——证据强度弱（单频点单负载，无全谱误差曲线）；XOR 测相位 + 整流测幅值是低成本方案的可行范式。
- **killRisk 判断：无威胁**。议题（解调架构选择）与 P1 三主张（RC 网络消去条件/Kelvin 地板/衰减上界）无交集；其对 SD 失配误差的陈述方向上支持"失配主导精度"的一般论点，不与 P1 任何主张竞争。
