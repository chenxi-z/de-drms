## 核心命题（原文）

1. **【答①·桥平衡条件的基准表述】** "A Wheatstone bridge is balanced when no current flows in the detector which occurs when Rx/Rs = Ra/Rb or Rx = RsRa/Rb. This is the balance equation and it is independent of the input signal level (if all arms are linear) and the detector sensitivity, and it remains unchanged if the source and detector are interchanged. The unknown is measured only by the values of other resistors, one of which is varied to achieve the balance."（p.4）——平衡式对激励幅度、检测器灵敏度不变；**未在此句提频率**。
2. **【答①·复阻抗推广】** "For a complex impedance the bridge balance equation now became ZX/ZS = ZA/ZB."（p.10，Heaviside 1892）；Maxwell-Wien 例 "Rx + jωLx = RaRb(Gs + jωCs)"，"This could be separated into separate real and imaginary balance conditions"（p.10）。
3. **【答①·频率相关的反例对照】** "If the 'unknown', CX, and its loss are represented by an equivalent parallel capacitance and resistance, the balance equation of Wien's bridge is frequency dependent (see equations in figure 1-17)."（p.10）——普通 RC 桥平衡频率相关；这句与 #4 合起来是"变压器比例臂为何频率上干净"的对照证据。
4. **【答①·匝比=阻抗比的原话】** "The first uses the pair of inductively coupled windings as ratio arms. **Ideally N1/N2 = Zx/Zs** but the winding impedances, resistance and leakage inductance, do affect the balance somewhat but the effect can be very small if high-permeability toroidal cores are used (µ values greater than 10⁵ are possible) and if the windings are carefully made."（p.33）；双比例桥 "the balance equation depends on two winding ratios: Zx/Zs = (N1/N2)(N3/N4)"（p.33–34）；Hill（AC Kelvin）桥 "the ratio N3/N4 should be set equal to N1/N2. Note that 4-terminal connections are made to both Zx and Zs."（p.34）。
5. **【答①·频率不进平衡式、但进误差项】** 附录 A："However, if z1 and z2 are very small compared to Z12, the transformer can provide extremely accurate ratios. This can be accomplished by twisting the windings together and using a high-permeability core."（p.60）；"Note that for a transformer with an iron core, M12 and R12 are nonlinear and not constant with frequency, signal level or temperature."（p.60）。**三篇通读结论：'与频率无关'没有人写成一句话；证据=平衡量是匝比（几何常数）+ 频率只经绕组阻抗/铁芯损耗扰动比值，且扰动有明确小量条件 z1,z2 ≪ Z12。**
6. **【答③·谱系主线之一：Kelvin 四端 + 辅助臂分压】** "He developed his 'New Electrodynamic Balance', now called the Kelvin (or Thomson) Bridge or Double Bridge (figure 1-4), that removes the error caused by the resistance, Ry, of the connection between the resistors being compared (called the 'yoke') with a second pair of ratio arms, RA' and RB', that divide the voltage drop across Ry proportionately so that at balance RX/RS = RA/RB = RA'/RB'. He described this bridge in 1862. Note that there are four connections to both RX and RS and the resistances of the connecting leads and contacts are placed in the other circuit branches where they have little effect."（p.4）；"The Kelvin Bridge was the first to make such 'four-terminal' connections and as a result they are sometimes referred to as 'Kelvin connections'."（p.5）
7. **【答③·谱系主线之二：Wagner 保护地 + 对偶自白】** "Karl Wagner made an important contribution to bridge measurements when he introduced the 'Wagner Ground' (or 'Wagner Earth') in 1911... More generally it can be used to make guarded, three-terminal measurements such as those on shielded three-terminal capacitors... When the auxiliary arm, C2, is adjusted so that the point P is at ground potential, Cb has no effect and Cx is measured directly."（p.13）；"It is interesting to note that a resistance bridge with a Wagner balance is the topological dual of the Kelvin bridge (figure 1-4) which also has two auxiliary arms and requires two balance adjustments."（p.13）——**"桥臂平衡 vs 端子结构"的对偶性被作者亲手点破**。
8. **【答③·谱系主线之三：TRA 是"结构自带保护"的终点】** "An important development in precision ac bridges was the use of a pair of inductively-coupled bridge arms, or 'transformer ratio arms', which have the advantages of a precise and constant ratio and of high tolerance to shunt loading"（p.32）；Blumlein 归属："it was the versatile genius Alan Blumlein who first clearly stated the advantages of using coupled ratio arms in a four-arm bridge. A figure from one of his patents shows a guarded measurement of a three-terminal capacitor (see figure 3-20)."（p.33，ref 3.18 = British Patent 323,037 "Alternating Bridge Circuits"）；"The inherent guarding of transformer-ratio-arm bridges eventually made such guard balances unnecessary."（p.24）；"The inherent guarding capability of a transformer-ratio-arm bridge allowed them to make 'in-situ' or 'in-circuit' measurements as long as all circuit paths shunting the DUT were guarded."（p.36）。
9. **【答③·概念位移一句】** 早期三绕组电路 "the pair of balanced coils was probably thought of more as a differential detector, analogous to Becquerel's differential galvanometer (figure 1-2), than as the ratio arms of a bridge."（p.32）——同一拓扑从"差分检测器"改读为"桥比例臂"，是整个谱系的认知转折点。
10. **【答③·消差工具箱=交换/替代/辅助臂/端子结构，唯独没有"匹配差分网络"】** Carey Foster："Two measurements were made with the standard and unknown interchanged thus canceling the effects of extraneous resistance and voltages as long as the resistances of the mercury cup contacts used for the connections remained constant."（p.5）；Kohlrausch 重叠分流："to avoid errors due to differences in the resistances of the two galvanometer coils, they were ingeniously interchanged in a manner that kept the connection resistances constant. The average of the two measurements could be very precise."（p.6）；到数字时代仪器分类轴仍是端子数："most all have extremely wide range of R, C and L, good 3-terminal (guarding) and 4-terminal ('Kelvin') capability."（p.55）。
11. **【答④·反例搜索结果】** 全文 grep "mismatch / common-mode / ladder" **零命中**；无任何"无源 RC 差分梯形失配→CM 转 DM 消去条件"。最接近的三处：①数字表前端误差公式（杂散电容负载项）："The error due to CL is ωCLRS/K where K is the open-loop at the test frequency."（p.49）；②Lynch 代换桥靠**拓扑摆放**使对地导纳不进平衡条件："It has the advantage of tolerating extreme loading to ground on either side of the DUT because these admittances are placed directly across either the source or the detector where they don't affect the balance conditions."（p.34）；③Wagner 让 Cb "has no effect"（p.13）。三者都是"寄生不进平衡式/由保护消掉"，不是"失配转换项配平消去"。
12. 时代收尾："To my knowledge no new, commercial, true (nulled) bridge, manual or automatic, has been introduced for many years."（p.57）；对 TRA 的保值评价："Manual transformer-ratio-arm (TRA) bridges apply higher signal levels and thus are less affected by noise and they have very good ratio accuracy. Thus older manual precision capacitance bridges, such as the GR 1615 and 1616, are still used for precision capacitance calibrations."（p.56）。

## 关键数值（原文）

- 变压器臂：高磁导环芯 µ > 10⁵ 可得（p.33）；GR 1605-A "one of the first instruments to use a high-permeability toroidal transformer with twisted-pair windings for its precise 1:1 ratio arms"（p.41）
- 精密史里程碑：Garton 1940 桥可测损耗角 **2 µrad**（p.34）；电容高度计检测 **1 aF**（p.34）；Thompson–Lampard 1956 可计算电容 <1 pF ⇒ "accurate measurements required good guarding and precise ratios were needed to extend the calibrations"（p.34）；NBS 绝对电容桥 8 只十进电容 **10 aF–100 pF**（p.34）；GR 1615/1616 通行各标准实验室（p.34）
- Guildline 电流比较仪（dc TRA）："RxIx = RsIs = RsIxNx/Ns or Rx = RsNx/Ns"（p.36）
- 数字时代精度：GR/QT 1689/1693 "a standard deviation at 1 kHz of about 2 ppm for a 1 second measurement... 3σ comparisons to 1 ppm by averaging fifty measurements"（p.56）；GR 1693 .02% @1 kHz、12 Hz–200 kHz（p.54）；Andeen-Hagerling 2500A "3 ppm accuracy and .07 ppm resolution at 1 kHz... 8½ digit"（p.56）；速度纪录 5.6 ms（Agilent E4980A，p.55）
- GR 1685 前端负载误差式 ωCLRS/K（p.49）；4271 用高环路增益"桥"把结点拉到地、只改保护不改测量（p.49）
- 附录 B/C：RLC 通用桥与微处理器表清单（p.61–64），可作机型普查索引

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照

- **P1（失配消去条件）**：③+④ 合并 = **"为什么没人做"的总图证据**。Hall 展示的 200 年误差消去工具箱只有五件：替代法、交换取均（Carey Foster/Kohlrausch/双替代）、辅助平衡臂（Kelvin yoke、Wagner ground）、端子结构（2T/3T/4T/guard）、比值标准（匝比、标准电阻）；**没有任何一处需要"匹配无源 RC 差分网络、把 CM 转成 DM 再消掉"**。且 TRA 把比例问题釜底抽薪（N1/N2=Zx/Zs，p.33）后，匹配差分滤波臂的需求在计量主线里根本不会出现。P1 论文可引 Fig 1-4（Kelvin）、Fig 1-23（Wagner）、Fig 3-21（五种 TRA 接法）三张图为谱系总图。注意 #11① 的 ωCLRS/K 是杂散电容**负载**误差（与 Whitlock 2004 的 Zcm 载荷通道同族），不是失配转换项。
- **P2（Kelvin 地板）**：Kelvin 四端（p.4–5）→ 电位计法（Thomas）→ 现代表"4-terminal ('Kelvin') capability"（p.55）构成完整谱系；Hall 的仪器史本身就是按端子结构分类史，是 P2"结构类是主导轴"的通史级证据。Hill 桥注记 "4-terminal connections are made to both Zx and Zs"（p.34）说明四端结构一路跟进 AC/TRA 时代。
- **P3（AAF 衰减上界）**：无（通史不涉现代 AAF；仅 RF 桥/Q 表等远缘内容）。
