- T. K. Bera, Nagaraju Jampana, "A Multifrequency Constant Current Source Suitable for Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT)," *Proc. 2010 Int. Conf. on Systems in Medicine and Biology*, IIT Kharagpur, India, pp. 278–283. DOI: 10.1109/icsmb.2010.5735387. Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore。

- 定位：**测量链自研文（EIT 多频恒流源）**——虽归 H6 文件夹，实为 EIT 仪表硬件文：MAX038 VCO + AD829 改进 Howland VCCS + 模拟多路复用 + NI DAQ。对本轴重点取证：测量硬件清单、定性前端误差对策（共模/失配/50Hz）、四端测量协议。

## 核心命题（原文）
1. **总命题：低成本 1 MHz 多频恒流源适配医学 EIT（p.278, Abstract）**：
   > "A multifrequency constant current source is developed for medical EIT and the boundary data of a practical phantom is studied. … Results show that the developed current source efficiently generate constant current with minimal amount of noise at different frequency levels."
   动机（p.278）：> "The available current injectors are either low frequency [15-20] systems or high frequency systems controlled by DSP [21]. In this context a low cost multifrequency constant current source with 1 MHz bandwidth is developed for electrical impedance tomography."
2. **★电路拓扑：VCO + 改进 Howland 电压控流源（p.280, §II.C）**：
   > "It consists of a variable frequency Voltage Controlled Oscillator (VCO) (Fig.-3) and a modified Howland Voltage Control Current Source (VCCS). … VCO is developed with is a high-frequency, precision function generator IC, MAX038 [26] … A sinusoidal voltage signal of a very high bandwidth (10Hz-10MHz) is obtained at the VCO output and fed to a VCCS which is basically a Modified Howland Current Generator (Fig.-6). VCCS is developed with AD829 IC (Analog Devices Inc.) [27]. The AD829 is a low noise, high speed op amp with custom compensation that provides the user with gains of ±1 to ±20 while maintaining a bandwidth greater than 50 MHz."
   （**改进 Howland 是 EIT 恒流源的主流拓扑**——注意与 DE-DRMS 否决 Howland 的前提不同：EIT 不需要"同一电流过样品与采样电阻"的比值前提。）
3. **★前端误差对策（定性）：信号调理 = 仪表放大 + 50 Hz 陷波；开关 = 高速模拟多路复用（p.281, §II.D）**：
   > "A signal conditioner block [28] is used with an instrumentation amplifier and a 50 Hz notch filter to increase the Signal to Noise Ratio (SNR). An electrode switching module is developed using high speed analog multiplexers (CD4067BE) [29] for injecting current with opposite method."
4. **★共模误差的域内对策：中央共模电极接地（p.281, §II.D）**：
   > "A common mode electrode (CME) [30] of cylindrical shape is placed at the phantom centre and connected to the ground point of the EIT hardwires to reduce the common mode error [31] of the electronic circuits."
   （**EIT 域承认共模误差是实际问题，但对策是域技巧（CME）而非前端 CMRR 定量设计**——无任何 CMRR 数值。）
5. **★电极阻抗失配对策：等长引线（p.281, §II.D）**：
   > "All the lead wires are of equal lengths for getting an identical impedance path for each electrode to reduce the mismatch in electrode impedances."
   （失配问题被点名，但用布线工艺缓解，无失配-共模转换的定量分析。）
6. **四端测量协议 + 商品 DAQ（p.281, §II.E）**：
   > "A USB based high speed data acquisition system consists of a NI USB-6251 DAQ card is used for boundary data acquisition through a multiplexer board connected to the surface electrodes. Data acquisition software is written in LabVIEW … 1 mA, sinusoidal current signal is injected to the phantom boundary at four different frequencies."
   （电流经一对电极注入、电压在其余边界电极测取（对置注入协议，Fig.-2）——**系统级四端/Kelvin 结构**：电流路径与电压测量电极天然分离。）

## 关键数值（原文）
- 电流 **1 mA** 正弦；频率 **100 Hz–1 MHz** 可注入，实测边界数据取 **20 / 50 / 100 / 200 kHz** 四频点（p.281）。
- VCO 输出带宽 **10 Hz–10 MHz**；VCCS 用 AD829（带宽 >50 MHz、增益 ±1–±20）（p.280）。
- VCCS 频响：**"the voltage across a 1 kΩ load remains constant (1000 mV) up to 1 MHz"**（p.282）——即 1 mA @ 1 kΩ 恒流至 1 MHz。
- VCO 输出 FFT 无杂散谐波（"free from undesired harmonics"，p.282，无数值）。
- 体模：16 电极、228 mm 内径浅槽、SS304 电极（100 µm 薄片）、0.05% (w/v) KCl 溶液、聚丙烯圆柱异质体（p.281）；完整数据集 128 个电压/16 电极系统，前 8 个投影足够成像（互易性原理，p.282）。
- **无 CMRR、输出阻抗、噪声密度、失配统计等任何定量指标**——"minimal amount of noise" 仅为定性表述。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件）**：**无**。交流激励（10 Hz–10 MHz）+ Howland VCCS，全文无直流通道路径、无消去结构；与 G_DC≡0 议题不相交（EIT 以交流阻抗成像为本分）。
- **P2（Kelvin 结构地板）**：**中（取证）**。四端结构在 EIT 是系统级默认（电流电极/电压电极分离，对置注入协议）——**"Kelvin 结构是产业级通行做法"的又一域证据（EIT 侧）**；共模误差被承认且以 CME 接地缓解（p.281），但**无 CMRR 地板、无失配→共模差模转换的任何定量**——与 P2"结构地板需定量"的主张形成正反对照：结构被用、地板未被量化。
- **P3（衰减可达上界）**：**无**（仅 50 Hz 陷波一处滤波，无衰减指标）。
- **方法学（门槛化）**：**低**。逐项功能验证（VCO FFT、VCCS 频响、四频点边界数据）是"能工作"级验收，无精度判据。
- **killRisk 判断：无**。对 P1/P2/P3 无威胁；其 Howland 用法与 DE-DRMS 否决 Howland 的理由不冲突（前提不同：EIT 无"同一电流"比值约束）。价值：EIT 域测量硬件清单 + 共模/失配的域内定性对策样本。
