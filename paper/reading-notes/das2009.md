## 核心命题（原文）
1. **拓扑综合的两步框架 + 图语法生成晶体管级拓扑**（p.1）：> "It is essentially a two step process — Topology formation followed by Sizing of the topology."（自动模拟综合=拓扑生成+尺寸化两步）；> "Graph-grammar based structure synthesis has been successfully applied to domains like sheet metal component design [9], cellular graph design [10] and epicyclic gear train design [11]. To the best of our knowledge, there has been no corresponding work on automated analog design till date."（作者的首创性声明只限"图语法用于模拟电路自动设计"这一方法域。）
2. **多目标环节由 NSGA-II 承担（两级 NSGA-II）**（p.1）：> "most analog designs are multi-objective optimization problems that often involve conflicting objectives. In this regard, the Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm (NSGA-II) [12] has been adopted in this framework."（每个拓扑先由 NSGA-II 尺寸化出一组 Pareto 解，再对全部已尺寸化拓扑做第二次 NSGA-II 排序形成新优化前沿——Fig.1 流程图：> "NSGA-II based circuit sizing for each topology (HSPICE based performance evaluation)" → > "NSGA-II based sorting of sized circuit topologies and formation of new optimization front"。）
3. **对 GP 结构综合的对比表（Table I）**（p.3）：> "Generally requires millions of designs to arrive at the final design."（GP 一侧）vs > "Thousands of designs are sufficient to generate the desired solution."（本文一侧）；另有四条优势：产生式规则 design-independent、自动生成子电路块、叶节点直接给出 SPICE 网表免解析器、电路规模可控。
4. **子电路性能拟合是按子树规模比例分摊的启发式，非灵敏度**（p.4，Fig.4）：> "F (child_ckt_portion) = Ȗ * F (parent_ckt_portion)"（抽取字符，原式为 γ 系数），> "Quantitatively, Ȗ = Fraction of size of child (i.e. no. of leafnodes / no. of design components) with respect to parent"（块库的 gross fitness 更新假设"性能按子树元件数占比线性分摊"——这是与灵敏度/统计设计路线最鲜明的对照：无任何一阶灵敏度或统计传播）。作者自认局限：> "Following [7], the assumption is claimed to be valid in this evolutionary CAD framework, where we are unaware of the different underlying design heuristics and equations."
5. **拓扑质量必须在尺寸化后评判**（p.4）：> "The quality of an analog circuit topology may only be judged after the topology has been sized appropriately [14]."（框架性命题：拓扑与尺寸不可分离评估。）
6. **能同时产出"书本电路"与"新颖电路"**（p.5）：> "Unlike previous works [5], [6], our tool produces both manual-like designs or bookish circuits, as well novel topologies."（p.5；摘要 p.1：> "we are capable of generating both manual-like designs (bookish circuits) as well as novel designs (unfamiliar circuits) for multi-objective analog circuit design benchmarks."）

## 关键数值（原文）
- 运行参数（p.5–6）：工艺 0.18 µm；sizer 每拓扑 48 条染色体 × 50 代；拓扑综合 100 代 × 100 条染色体（运放）/ 50 代 × 100 条（VCO）；每代 30% 电路由块库生成、70% 由交叉变异产生（p.5）：> "in each generation, 30% of the circuits are formed using library blocks and the rest 70% through crossover and mutation mechanisms."
- 变量范围（p.5）：> "W tran → 0.36:0.18:80μ; V bias → 0:0.1:5.0V"（min:granularity:max），> "All transistor lengths are fixed at 0.36μ for ease in sizing."；负载 CL = 10 pF。
- 规格（Table II，p.6）：Design-I 运放——> "DC Gain ≥ 40 dB, 3dB freq. ≥ 50 MHz, UGF (Bandwidth) ≥ 5 GHz, Phase margin: 45 − 60◦"；Design-II VCO——> "Center oscillation frequency ≥ 2 GHz, Tuning range ≥ 0.1 GHz"。
- 结果（Table II，p.6）：Pareto 最优设计数 15（运放）/ 6（VCO）；两个新颖运放（p.6 图注）：> "Gain = 42.34 dB, UGF = 7.98 GHz, 3dB frequency = 97.96 MHz, Phase margin = 47°"（a）、> "Gain = 42.15 dB, UGF = 9.2 GHz, 3dB frequency = 90.4 MHz , Phase margin = 56°"（b）。
- VCO 结果（p.6）：> "The design produces an oscillation frequency of 4.17 GHz with Vc = 3.5 V."；> "Through variation of Vc within an appropriate 2V range, the tuning range obtained is 0.12 GHz."
- 对比量级（p.5）：> "the GP-based technique in [5] used 640, 000 solutions per generation and generated a single 5dB amplifier circuit after 45 generations."（Koza GP 的量级 vs 本文千级）；[6]（p.6）：> "This is better than [6], which synthesized a single similar design taking 40 generations, each consisting of 600 chromosomes."
- 块库复用加速（p.5）：第二次综合复用首轮块库，> "12 well-compliant optimal design solutions are obtained only within 50 generations."
- ⚠️ 原文笔误存档（p.4）：> "Also, HSPCIE is used for performance evaluation"（HSPCIE 为 HSPICE 之笔误，正文两处拼写并存）。

## 对 P1/P2/P3 的对照
- **P1（消去条件 G_DC=H_N−H_P≡0）**：低。拓扑由文法随机生长、尺寸由进化搜索，全文无传递函数恒等式、无元件条件、无差分结构消去分析；基准电路是运放/VCO，不含无源网络。
- **P2（Kelvin 结构地板 ≈56dB）**：无。不涉共模抑制、接地结构或布线拓扑的抑制机理。
- **P3（平坦度约束衰减可达上界 = Bode-Fano 亲缘）**：低。本批重点①③的答案：**das2009 不触及滤波器多目标 Pareto 框架**——两个 benchmark 均为晶体管级电路，无滤波器算例、无频响平坦度-衰减权衡、无匹配界；其多目标部分只是 NSGA-II 的标准使用（与 lyu2018 同属进化/代理搜索家族）。其子电路性能分摊（γ=规模占比）是**反例对照**：进化 CAD 在"不知道解析方程"时用启发式分摊，恰反衬 P3 选用灵敏度/统计设计的解析路线的必要性。
- **方法学（D1–D10 门槛化）**：低-中。可作"自动多目标模拟综合"的背景引用（DATE 系），但与门槛化"解析判据+逐条验证"精神相反，属于搜索式方法学——引用时应放在"对比方法"而非"支撑方法"位置。
- **killRisk**：无威胁。四条主张均与其无重叠；其"till date 无同域工作"的首创声明仅限图语法拓扑综合，不波及 P1。
- **线索存档**：同作者同年前作 [8] = Das & Vemuri, "An automated **passive analog circuit** synthesis framework using genetic algorithms," ISCAS 2008（参考文献列表 p.6）——标题直指**无源模拟电路自动综合**，与 P1/P3 的域更近，若 P1 方法学需要"无源网络自动综合"先例应补读该篇（本 PDF 未含）。
