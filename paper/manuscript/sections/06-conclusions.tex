\section{Conclusions}

We established a recursive coefficient representation and positive-component recovery test for grounded multistage differential resistor-capacitor ladders. For a fixed $N$-stage reference branch, open-circuit matching left $N$ local degrees of freedom, one positive resistive termination left $N-1$, and matching complete transfers at two distinct known terminations required identical components. A rational-load kernel characterized how load type changed the matching dimension. The AC checks covered one to twelve stages.

For calibrated ratio measurements, matched normalized transfers rejected excitation variation exactly for constant resistance. Varying resistance produced an excitation-weighted history average. The signed first-order error combined resistance tracking with $-R(\dot I/I)(\tau_P-\tau_N)$, and three- and four-stage transient cases verified the resulting distinctions. The load-shift formula also predicted the observed common-mode step pulse. The network conditions specify nominal cancellation, while the convolution model specifies the dynamic quantity preserved by that design.
