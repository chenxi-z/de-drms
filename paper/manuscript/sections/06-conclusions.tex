\section{Conclusions}

We established termination-dependent cancellation conditions for grounded second-order differential resistor-capacitor ladders. For a fixed reference branch, open-circuit cancellation admitted a two-parameter matching family; one positive resistive termination admitted a one-parameter family; and equality of the complete transfer functions at two distinct positive resistive terminations required identical corresponding components. Positive-component recovery conditions and explicit branch constructions made each result directly checkable. The analytical predictions were reproduced in 37 AC simulation cases, and an output bridging capacitor preserved the common-mode rejection ratio through a shared loading factor in the two differential-output gains.
