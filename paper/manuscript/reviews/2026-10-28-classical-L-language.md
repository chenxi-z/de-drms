- **Why C rather than B:** restoring a validity condition changes the claim’s scope, even though the edit is short.

### L-03 — C — Categorical non-preservation exceeds the displayed first-order mechanism

### L-04 — C — Two-load conclusion leaves convention and termination class ambiguous

- **Location:** `/media/anning/data/Project/DE-DRMS/paper/manuscript/sections/05-discussion.tex:3`.
- **Original:** “Two distinct known terminations fix the complete polynomial pair and therefore the components.”
- **Evidence:** L4 and ER-L01. In the body, two distinct known **rational** admittances determine the components **under raw matching** (`sections/02a-general-order-theory.tex:128`); the normalized two-load proof is stated for **resistive-load responses** (`:90`).
