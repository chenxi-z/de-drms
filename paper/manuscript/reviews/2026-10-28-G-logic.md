## What the present evidence does and does not establish

I ran `python3 paper/derivations/dynamic_ratio.py`: all 11 checks passed, including units, signed first-order term, normalized/raw distinction, load-shift sign, step peak/energy and quadrature. This is a targeted consistency check, not proof of prior-art novelty or an exhaustive general-order audit. The corrected transient results support the stated approximately 5.59% and 8.63% first-order relative RMS discrepancies and maximum native/exact ratio disagreement around 1.23e-6 ohm. Thus it would be wrong to report a missing sign correction, absent transient verification, or a contradiction between raw and calibrated matching.

The 84-case/37-case native agreement demonstrates implementation/model consistency over the selected circuits. It is not a comparison against a competing design method and should not be sold as such. The exponential history examples demonstrate a real distinction between exact current cancellation at constant R and history weighting at varying R. A fast pulse is checked exactly rather than forced into the moment approximation; this is appropriate.

The paper can be a theory paper. Mandatory completion is a defensible novelty boundary, an explicit research gap, and claims proportional to available evidence. A compact design example makes its engineering relevance much stronger; if that payoff is retained, it needs actual support. Full bench work, stochastic noise, parasitic/SC receiver models, temperature drift, generalized topologies and normalized-family dimension formulas are optional extensions under the current stated ideal grounded-LTI scope. They become required only if the manuscript expands its claims into those domains.

Recommended repair order: (1) primary-source proposition comparison; (2) rewrite gap/contribution boundary from that comparison; (3) one specification-to-design comparison or narrow the design claims; (4) quantify or narrowly label the moment approximation; (5) expose the existing verification matrix/data; (6) re-review abstract/discussion/conclusions against the repaired chain.
