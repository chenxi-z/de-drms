# Manuscript figure manifest

All figures are original artwork generated by `../tools/generate_figures.py`, adapted from the executed howtoSCI `tpl_curve_matrix.py`. The evidence is analytical calculation and archived LTspice AC output, not laboratory measurements. Each file is a standalone figure with a 7.5 × 5.625 cm canvas; do not combine independently cropped panels into one figure environment. SVG retains text, PDF is the manuscript vector asset, and PNG is 600 dpi.

Common normalization: R* = 1 kΩ, C* = 10 nF, x = sR*C*. Branch coefficients P=(3,1,2,1), Q=(3,1,5,2), T=(2.9,1,2,2), ordered (a,b,c,d). See the manuscript component table for physical constructions.

## Fig_01_circuit — Methods

**English caption.** Two grounded second-order RC branches and their output terminations. Each ladder contains two series resistors and two shunt capacitors. The shunt loads connect each output to ground; the optional capacitor Cd bridges the two outputs. The dotted blue route identifies the optional bridge. Input voltages are supplied by ideal sources referenced to ground.

**中文图注。** 两条对地二阶 RC 支路及其输出端接。每条梯形支路由两个串联电阻和两个对地电容构成。负载分别连接输出节点与地；可选电容 Cd 跨接两个输出。蓝色点线标出可选跨接路径。输入由对地理想电压源驱动。

## Fig_02_positive_region — Coefficient recovery / analytical results

**English caption.** Admissible positive-component region for the fixed open-circuit coefficients a=3 and b=1. The shaded interior satisfies c(3d−c)>d² with d>0. Boundary lines are excluded. P and Q are two different realizations inside the same open-circuit transfer family. Axis coordinates are normalized coefficients.

**中文图注。** 开路系数固定为 a=3、b=1 时的正元件可实现区域。阴影内部满足 d>0 及 c(3d−c)>d²，边界线不属于可实现区域。P、Q 是同一开路传递函数族内的两组不同实现。坐标为归一化系数。

Source: exact positivity inequalities and P/Q coefficients, not a statistical estimate. Region dimensions apply for a fixed reference branch.

## Fig_03_termination_transfer — Results

**English caption.** Magnitude responses of P and Q under open-circuit and 10 kΩ shunt-load conditions. The open-circuit responses coincide, whereas loading separates the two branch responses. Lines are the analytical transfer functions and open circles are archived LTspice samples. R*=1 kΩ and C*=10 nF throughout.

**中文图注。** P、Q 支路在开路及 10 kΩ 对地负载条件下的幅频响应。两条开路响应重合，接入负载后响应分离。实线为解析传递函数，空心圆为已有 LTspice 仿真数据。全图采用 R*=1 kΩ、C*=10 nF。

Native cases: a_p_oc, a_p_10k, a_q_10k; 145 original points each, every twelfth point displayed. The analytical curves use 901 logarithmically spaced frequencies from 10 Hz to 10 MHz. The P=Q open equality was independently verified in the underlying 37-case evidence.

## Fig_04_output_impedance — Results

**English caption.** Open-circuit output-impedance magnitudes of P, Q, and T with the ideal input voltage sources set to zero. P and Q share the same open-circuit voltage transfer but have different output impedances. Curves are calculated from the four-coefficient branch representation.

**中文图注。** 理想输入电压源置零后，P、Q、T 支路的开路输出阻抗幅值。P 与 Q 的开路电压传递函数相同，输出阻抗不同。曲线由支路的四系数表达式计算。

## Fig_05_load_specific_match — Results

**English caption.** Relative magnitude response of T to P at two common shunt loads. The ordinate is 20 log10|HT/HP|; zero indicates equal magnitudes. P and T have identical complete transfer functions at 10 kΩ. A 5 kΩ load produces a frequency-dependent mismatch. Lines are analytical responses and open circles are archived LTspice samples.

**中文图注。** 两种相同对地负载下 T 相对于 P 的幅值响应。纵轴为 20 log10|HT/HP|，零值表示幅值相等。P、T 在 10 kΩ 负载下具有相同的完整传递函数；负载改为 5 kΩ 后出现随频率变化的失配。实线为解析结果，空心圆为已有 LTspice 仿真数据。

Native cases: a_p_10k, a_t_10k, a_p_5k, a_t_5k. This magnitude plot illustrates the complete-function algebraic result; magnitude equality alone is not used to establish transfer equality.

## Fig_06_bridge_gains — Results

**English caption.** Analytical differential-output gains of the loaded P/Q pair with and without an output bridging capacitor. DM denotes a unit differential input; CM→DM denotes the differential output under a unit common-mode input. The open markers identify the 820 pF curves and are sampled analytical values. Both branch shunt loads are 10 kΩ.

**中文图注。** P/Q 支路对在有、无输出跨接电容时的解析差分输出增益。DM 表示单位差模输入；CM→DM 表示单位共模输入产生的差分输出。空心标记用于识别 820 pF 曲线，标记点同样来自解析计算。两条支路的对地负载均为 10 kΩ。

All four curves are analytical; no marker is labelled as a measurement or independent simulation here. Changes in both gains share the same complex loading factor.

## Fig_07_bridge_cmrr — Results

**English caption.** Common-mode rejection ratio of the loaded P/Q pair. The analytical curves for Cd=0 and 820 pF coincide because the shared loading factor cancels from GDD/GDC. Open circles are calculated from the archived common-mode and differential-mode LTspice output differences with Cd=820 pF. Both shunt loads are 10 kΩ.

**中文图注。** 负载条件下 P/Q 支路对的共模抑制比。Cd=0 和 820 pF 的解析曲线重合，因为共同的负载因子在 GDD/GDC 中约去。空心圆由 Cd=820 pF 时已有共模、差模 LTspice 输出差值计算。两条对地负载均为 10 kΩ。

Native cases: v2_pq_10k_cd_cm and v2_pq_10k_cd_dm. CMRR=20 log10|GDD/GDC|. These cases have nonzero common-to-differential conversion; no numerical cancellation floor is interpreted as physical infinite CMRR.

## Dynamic extension: Fig_08–Fig_10

Rebuild with `python3 paper/manuscript/tools/generate_dynamic_figures.py --verification '/absolute/path/to/verification'`. Numerical sources are the independently corrected `2026-10-28-codex-audit/dynamic-corrections` data and the original `2026-10-28-dynamic/cases/T2_cm_pulse` raw evidence. No historical files are modified and no new simulation is run by the plotting script. Each figure has English and Chinese TeX wrappers (`fig08-en.tex` / `fig08-zh.tex`, etc.) with aligned labels and captions.

- **Fig_08_dynamic_weighting**: three- and four-stage *matched* ladders, the same exponentially changing resistance and two different positive current histories. Exact loaded determinants are evaluated independently, including the downstream loading terms. The plotted subtraction is the α=1000/s ratio minus the α=0 ratio, rather than a claim of current immunity. Open markers come from interpolated native ratios. At 2.5 ms the analytical differences are 5.1137664 Ω (N=3) and 11.9446091 Ω (N=4).
- **Fig_09_dynamic_slew**: a constant 100 kΩ resistance, 100 Hz sinusoidal positive current, and 10% increase in the reference ladder's final capacitor. Exact steady-state phasor division, the signed first-moment expression, and native simulation markers are shown separately. The native-time first-order relative RMS discrepancy is 8.6342%; the exact phasor/native maximum discrepancy is 1.2210×10⁻⁶ Ω. The independent phasor evaluation agrees with the archived analytical curve within 5.83×10⁻¹¹ Ω.
- **Fig_10_dynamic_pulse**: positive `outP − outT` after the 1 V common-input edge under 5 kΩ loads. Native ASCII data are streamed, and the raw header's 0.5 ms Offset is applied. The analytical line includes the actual 10 ns finite rise, using the residues of +0.1/[(x²+3.2x+1.4)(x²+3.3x+1.4)] for the step response and averaging over the finite ramp. It agrees with all 7506 selected native points within 4.51×10⁻⁹ V. The native positive peak is 9.1976021 mV at 28.2174 μs after edge start.

`provenance_dynamic.json` contains streamed source hashes, actual exported SVG glyph-size measurements (including subscripts), unclipped marker checks, zero PNG border measurements, and legend/curve intersection checks. All plots use 7.3 pt base Times New Roman with custom Times math and independent single-panel figure environments. Figure 8 shows analytical/native ratio differences for changing resistance; Figure 9 treats constant resistance with channel mismatch. These are distinct conditions.
